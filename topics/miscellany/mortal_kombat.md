## Introduction

There exist several **Realms** created and overseen by the **Elder Gods**. Some of these Realms are:

- **Earth**: The human's world. Protected by **Raiden**, the God of Thunder.
- **Outworld**: Ruled by the emperor **Shao Kahn**
- **Netherrealm**: Volcanic afterlife comparable to Hell, ruled by the fallen Elder God **Shinnok**.
- **Khaosrealm**: Absolute anarchy. Floating rock formations. Inverted physics. Lack of government. Led by the anarchist **Havik**.
- **Orderrealm**: Strict authoritatian control. Advanced, urbanized society governed by **Hotaru**.
- **Edenia**: Lush, fertile, idyllic jungle resembling the Garden of Eden. Ruled by **King Jerrod**, **Queen Sindel**, and **Princess Kitana**.

Many realms were at war with each other, so the Gods decreed that any realm that wants to conquer another has to win ten consecutive **Mortal Kombat** tournaments.

The Elder God **Shinnok** tried to conquer the Earth realm, but Raiden defeated him, stripped him of his power, and exiled him to the Netherrealm. Shinnok built a team and took control of the Netherrealm with the help of his lieutenant **Quan Chi**.

Emperor **Shao Kahn** tried to conquer the Earth realm. He and his team won 9 tournaments. Then Raiden assembled a team of fighters to make the last stand for Earth.

## MK: Special forces

During a prision raid, **Kano**, the crime lord of the Black Dragon Empire, slaughters many Special Forces troops and escapes to the Outworld, where Kano discovers an artifact that allows him to travel between realms. However, he was followed and hunted down by the U.S. Army Major **Jax Briggs**, who uses the artifact to travel back to Earth and takes Kano to prison.

## MK mythologies: Sub-Zero

**Quan Chi** hires **Sub-Zero** to steal an old map from a Shaolin temple. Unbeknownst to Sub-Zero, **Scorpion** is also hired to recover the map. As a result, both fight for the map. Sub-Zero kills Scorpion and gets the map. Instead of handing over the map to Quan Chi, he uses it to get to a treasure: an amulet that belonged to Shinnok. However, Quan Chi appears and gets the amulet before Sub-Zero can get it.

Raiden, who is pretty pissed at Sub-Zero for handling the powerful amulet to the Earth enemies, sends him on a mission to retrieve the amulet. Sub-Zero travels to the Netherrealms, defeats Scorpion (now an undead shadow of his former self), and gets into Quan Chi's fortress. He gets the amulet and defeats Quan Chi. Then Shinnok appears, but Sub-Zero escapes to Earth and hands over the amulet to Raiden.

## Mortal Kombat

For 500 years, Shao Kahn and his team have won tournament after tournament, winning a total of 9 tournaments. The warrior **Goro** was the champion of these tournaments. The tournaments were hosted by the sorcerer **Shang Tsung**, Shao Kahn's right hand. 

Raiden assembled a team of fighters for the last tournament:

- Shaolin monk **Liu Kang** (main fighter)
- Movie star **Johnny Cage**
- Special Forces agent **Sonya Blade**
- Criminal mastermind **Kano**
- **Sub-Zero** (real name: Han)
- Undead assassin **Scorpion**

During the tournament, Sub-Zero was killed by Scorpion. Finally, Liu Kang killed Goro and won the tournament, saving the Earth from the Outworld's takeover.  

## Mortal Kombat 2

After losing the 10th tournament, and the death of Goro, Shao Kahn wanted to punish Shang Tsung. However, Shang convinced the emperor to let him organize another tournament in the Outworld. Raiden and his team agreed to the rematch and traveled to the Outworld.

After dying in the previous Mortal Kombat, Han descended to the Netherrealm, where he stripped of his humanity and became the shadow wraith **Noob Saibot**. His younger brother, **Qui Liang**, assumed his brother's former alter ego, Sub-Zero, and joined Raiden's team.

Liu Kang's best mate, **Kung Lao**, joins Raiden's team.

Finally, Liu Kang won the tournament.

## MK: Shaolin monks

This is a rerun of Mortal Kombat 2 events from the perspective of Liu Kang and Kung Lao.

## Mortal Kombat 3

Shao Kahn executes a plan that he had 10.000 years in the making for conquering Earth. He had a partner, the young queen **Sindel**, but she died long time ago. He planned to reincarnate her within the boundaries of Earth, allowing him to step through the dimensional gates to reclaim his queen, and seize the Earth.

Shao Kahn takes over his queen and the Earth realm. He strips the Earth of all human life by taking the souls of all humans. However, he cannot take the souls of the warriors chosen to represent Earth in a new Mortal Kombat, since they are protected by Raiden.

Liu Kang, who is a main target for Shao Kahn, join forces with Kung Lao and the **Rebellion** against the invaders. Kung Lao dies fighting Shao Kahn's forces. This event fuels Liu Kang's fury and he eventually defeats Shao Kahn.

Sindel was actually the wife of **Jerrod**, the benevolent king of Edenia, and both were parents of princess **Kitana**. Long time ago, Shao Kahn took over Edenia, used Sindel as a pawn in his quest for Earth, and took Kitana as his own daughter. However, after Shao Kahn's defeat, Sindel freed Earth and Edenia, and reunited with her daughter. Liu Kang ended up shacking up with Kitana.

The **Lin Kuei** was a clan of assassins to which Sub-Zero belongs. They decided to convert their five best human ninjas into soulless cyborgs: Sub-Zero, **Nightwolf**, **Sektor**, **Cyrax**, and **Smoke**. Sub-Zero refused to take part and tried to escape with his friend Smoke, but Smoke was captured. Sub-Zero left the clan and joined the Rebellion. By leaving the clan, he broke their sacred codes of honor, so he was marked for death. The clan turned the other four ninjas into cyborgs and sent them to find and kill Sub-Zero.

After Shao Kahn's defeat, **Sonya** convinces her superiors to form the OIA (Outerworld Investigation Agency), devoted to protect the Earth against possible future invasions from other realms.

## Mortal Kombat 4

The former Elder God **Shinnok** was confined to the Netherrealm. However, his lieutenant **Quan Chi** freed him. With the help of **Tanya**, an edenian traitor, Shinnok overtook Edenia and then waged war against the Elder Gods who banished him, including Raiden.

Sub-Zero was tricked by Quan Chi into believing Scorpion killed his family, so he tracked and fought Scorpion.

Sonya continues fighting the Black Dragon crime syndicate. She tracks down another of its members: **Jarek**. He almost killed Sonya, but Jax stepped in and dropped him from a cliff. They also discovered the cyborg assassin Cyrax malfunctioning in a desert, so they revived him, restored his humanity, and recruited him into the OIA.

Kung Lao, who was considered death, revealed that he just faked his death to live a life that was respectful to his ancestors. He joined the fight against Shinnok.

Raiden assembled a team to defeat Shinnok, who was eventually defeated by Liu Kang. After this, the Elder Gods granted Raiden full Elder God status, so he retires from his position of defender of Earth realm, handling over the role to his brother **Fujin**, the God of Wind.

## MK: Deadly alliance

The evil sorcerers Quan Chi and Shang Tsung formed an alliance after Quan Chi discovered an ancient tomb containing the mummified remains of the first Ourworld's emperor, the **Dragon King Onaga**, and his massive army. Before taking over the universe, they first decide to eliminate their biggest rivals: Shao Kahn and Liu Kang.

They fake allegiance to Shao Kahn and then both murder him. Then they travel to Earth realm and Shang Tsung kills Liu Kang. Then both go back to Outworld, and Quan Chi uses Shinnok's amulet (recovered in "MK mythologies: Sub-Zero") to open up a portal to the heavens called **Soulnado** from where they take souls to resurrect the Dragon King's army.

Raiden marshalled a team that then travelled to Outworld to stop the Deadly alliance. However, they failed, and Kung Lao, Kitana, Jax, and Sonya were killed. Then Raiden renounced his status as Elder God and rejoined the fight to take out the Deadly Alliance himself.

## MK: Deception

Raiden fought the Deadly Alliance, but he was defeated by its leaders, the sorcerers Quan Chi and Shang Tsung. Then the sourcerers turned on themselvers in a bid to claim the power of controlling the Dragon King's army. Quan Chi emerged victorious. However, he didn't realize that Onaga was resurrected. This forced Quan Chi, Shang Tsung, and Raiden to join forces to fight the Dragon King.

The fight was tought and Raiden decided to release his Godly Essence, which resulted in a big explosion that killed Quan Chi and Shang Tsung, but unfortunately Onaga was not killed.

Onaga, disguised as an emissary for the Elder Gods, tricked a boy called **Shujinko** into searching for six mistical kamidogu (God tools) to help him further cement his power. To help Shujinko, Onaga teached him how to mimic other fighting styles.

Raiden revived Liu Kang as a zombified husk of a monk with murderous instincts, totally unbefitting of a Shaolin monk. However, Liu Kang's soul had lived on and latched itself to a warrior called **Ermac**.

Two sides fought for supremacy: the surviving fighters of the Deadly Alliance Vs. Onaga and his supporters. With the help of **Mileena**, an edenian princess that pretended to be princess Kitana, Onaga drafted Edenia into his ranks. Onaga also resurrected all of the previously deceased combatants as undead warriors to fight for him, including Sonya, Kung Lao, Kitana, and Jax.

Eventually Shujinko found out about Onaga's deception. Ermac, together with Liu Kang's soul, helped to free the imprisoned souls of Onaga's undead army. Finally, Shujinko fought and defeated Onaga using the same skills that Onaga taught him.

## MK: Armageddon

Centuries ago, the Edenian sorcerer **Delia** foretold the apocalyptic end of all realms: the cumulative power of all the realm's fighters will eventually destabilize them and lead to its ultimate demise. To prevent this, her husband, the king and Elder God **Argus**, decided to organize a battle royale against **Blaze**, a man on fire. Blaze was created solely for the purpose of killing the fighters of all realms. However, if anyone but Argus' sons, **Taven** or **Dagon**, defeate Blaze, the apocalypse would be triggered. The battle takes place in the future, so Taven and Dagon awake in the future.

Non-canon ending: Taven defeats Blaze and becomes an Elder God.

Canon ending: Only Shao Kahn and Raiden survive the battle.

## MK Vs. DC universe

This is an alternative storyline on its own. The MK characters and DC heroes join forces to fight in a battle royale against the villains Shao Kahn (MK) and **Darkseid** (DC), who had fused into **Dark Kahn**. The only survivors are Raiden and Superman, who team up and defeat Dark Kahn.

## Mortal Kombat 9

Shao Kahn defeated Blaze (MK: Armageddon). As a result, he was granted god-like powers, which he uses to fight and defeat the last living combatant: Raiden. Before being killed, Raiden uses his own Elder God amulet to send a message back in time to his past self simply stating that "he must win" (to prevent the armageddon). This message reaches the Raiden from MK 1, but he doesn't understand who is "he". This event creates a whole new branch of the timeline, making MK 9 a soft reboot of the franchise that retells the events of MK 1, MK 2, and MK 3.

Back to the MK 1 events (Earth tournament), Raiden had a premonition of the future along with his future self's message. This damages his amulet. The next events unfold as they happened in MK 1, whereSub-Zero killed Scorpion, and Liu Kang killed Goro.

The MK 2 events (Outworld tournament) have some changes:

- Raiden rescues Smoke from becoming a Lin Kuei's cyborg at the expense of Sub-Zero.
- Raiden gives priority to Kung Lao over Liu Kang in the tournament. This results in Kung Lao defeating Shang Tsung and Quan Chi, but he's later killed by a surprise neck snap by Shao Kahn. This makes Liu Kang furiously attack Shao Kahn, who had to be nursed back to help by Quan Chi.

The MK 3 events (Earth invasion) have some changes:

- The Shao Kahn and Quan Chi alliance fast tracks the revival of his former queen Sindel, opening the door for the Earth invasion.
- Sindel kills most of heroes, including Jax, Sub-Zero, Smoke, and Kitana.
- Raidens discovers who is "he" in the message: Shao Kahn. If Shao Kahn invades Earth without winning via a Mortal Kombat, the Elder Gods would punish him. Thus, he surrenders to Shao Kahn, which makes the Elder Gods intervene to grant Raiden godly superpowers that he uses to destroy Shao Kahn.
- During the invasion, Liu Kang tried to attack Shao Kahn, but Raiden stopped him, accidentally killing him in the process.
- Quan Chi is revealed to be working with Shinnok, and Shao Kahn's destruction and Earth's devastation were parts of their plan.

## Mortal Kombat 10

Shinnok has launched a full scale invasion of Earth with his army of undead, which includes many deceased fighters from MK 9. Sonya and Johnny Cage hold back Shinnok's advances long enough for Raiden to imprison Shinnok in his amulet. They track Quan Chi back to the Netherrealm, where they restore some fighters to their human form, including Jax, Sub-Zero, and Scorpion. They overpower Quan Chi. Finally, peace descends across the realms.

Twenty years pass:

- Johnny and Sonya hook up, have a daughter called **Cassie**, and get divorced.
- Sub-Zero kills Sektor and reclaims his place as head of the Lin Kuei.
- Sub-Zero and Scorpion reconcile their rivalry after Sub-Zero discovers the treachery behind the death of Scorpion's family.

A new generation of fighters arise, including Cassie (Johnny-Sonya's daughter), **Jackie** (Jax's daughter), and **Kung Jin** (Kung Lao's nephew). They get involved in a civil war on Outworld, which is now ruled by **Kotal Kahn**. In all the ensuing chaos, the insectoid warrior **Devorah**, Kotal's 2nd in command, but actually a double agent working for Quan Chi, gets Shinnok's amulet. The heroes fight to prevent Quan Chi and Devorah from resurrecting Shinnok. They manage to kill Quan Chi, but Shinnok is revived anyway and he launches another invasion of Earth.

Shinnok gets to the **Jinsei chamber** (within Raiden's Sky Temple), which houses the Jinsei (the Earth realm's life force), and corrupts it, which turns him into a great demon god. However, Cassie defeats him. Then Raiden purifies the Jinsei by absorbing Shinnok's corruption, strips Shinnok of all his power, and gets Shinnok's amulet. The upshot of this is that the Netherrealm has new people in charge: the undead Liu Kang and Kitana.

## Mortal Kombat 11

After absorbing Shinnok's corruption, Raiden becomes corrupted (**Dark Raiden**), making him ruthless and vengeful. He decides to decapitate Shinnok, which causes Shinnok's mother, **Chronica**, the keeper of all time, to take revenge by erasing Raiden from all timelines. Chronica makes an alliance with the undead Liu Kang and Kitana, and then brings from the past the younger versions of various fighters, including Raiden, Liu Kang, Kitana, Johnny Cage, Jax, Scorpion, and Kung Lao. She also erases Dark Raiden from the current timeline. Young and old versions of various fighters get in touch.
