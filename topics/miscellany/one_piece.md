## Introduction

In the world there are many island and only one continent, the **Red Line**, which circles the planet from north to south. The **Grand Line** is an oceanic current that goes along the ecuator. The Red Line and the Grand Line surround the **4 seas**: North Blue, South Blue, West Blue, East blue (where this story begins). The **All blue** is a mythical sea somewhere. The Grand Line is surrounded by two parallel belts where there is no wind and no current (**Calm Belt**), home of huge sea monsters (**Sea Kings**). In the Red Line there's the **Reverse mountain**, which has 5 channels that connect the Grand Line and the 4 seas at the top, and serve as a point of access to any of these regions.

<br>![map_1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AnselmoGPP/know_base/master/topics/miscellany/resources/one_piece_2.png)

The **Devil's fruit** (Akuma no mi) is a rare type of fruit that gives powers to those who eat it, but take away their ability to swim. There're many types of them. The **Kairouseki** (seastone) is a crystallized form of the sea that temporarilly suppresses the powers of people that have eaten a devil's fruit.

**Gold D. Roger**, the King of Pirates, got arrested and executed by the **World Government**. Before dying, he revealed that he left his **One Piece** treasure (“everything this world has to offer”) at the Grand Line. Many pirates started looking for this treasure, initiating the **Great Pirate Era**.

The World Government is an alliance of 170 countries. Its highest authority is the **Gorousei** (The five elderly stars), a counsel of 5 elders, in **Mariejoa**, in the Sacred island. They control the **3 great powers**, which reign over the Grand Line, making it deadly for pirates. One of such powers are the **Sichibukai**, the 7 Warlords of the sea. This is a group of government-authorized pirates, since they pay a cut of the loot they get. Some of them are: Hawk-eye, Jimbei, Crocodile, Don Quixote Doflamingo, Bartholomew Kuma, Gecko Moria.

## New friends

(chapters 1-17)

**Monkey D. Luffy** was a boy that wanted to find the One Piece and become the King of Pirates. When he was a 7 yo kid living at **Foosha village**, in **Dawn island**, he met pirate **Shanks**, leader of the **Red Hair pirates**. They were carrying a Devil’s fruit (gum fruit), but Luffy ate it by mistake and became a very strong rubber person. Both became friends and Shanks gave Luffy his straw hat before going back to the sea.

Luffy (7 yo) befriended a kid called **Ace** (10 yo), son of Gold Roger, and both became sworn brothers. When Ace was 17 yo he left the island to become a pirate, and 3 years later (20 years after the beginning of the Great Pirate Era) Luffy tried to do the same. He got a ship but it was destroyed by a whirlpool and he was rescued by some passenger ship.

**Coby** was a boy that was captured by pirate Alvida one day while he was fishing, and was forced to serve at his ship for 2 years. Luffy defeated Alvida when she attacked the passenger ship where he was, and became friend of Coby.

**Roronoa Zoro** was a pirate hunter and great swordman (expert in the art of three swords) that was imprisioned at the **Navy base** city island (the Navy headquarters), for defying the Navy. The Navy was in charge of chasing pirates. Luffy was looking for a crew, so he rescued Zoro, and both defeated **Morgan**, the captain in charge of that base. Zoro joined Lufy, but Coby decided to join the Navy.

**Nami** was an 18 years old navigator who stole from pirates. Her goal was to get 100 million Berries for unknown reasons.

Luffy and Zoro left the island in a small boat and arrived to a town that was being pillaged by **Buggy the Clown**, a pirate who had eaten a Devil’s fruit (chop fruit), which allowed him to be cut into pieces and put together again. Both fought Buggy and his crew, and defeated them, and Luffy threw Buggy far away. Nami, who was trying to steal from Buggy, joined them. From Buggy they took a chart of the Grand Line (Buggy stole it from the Navy base island) and a treasure (~5 million berries) and they left the island with their small boat and Buggy’s boat. 

Then, they arrived to a village in an island where they met **Usopp**, the son of Yasopp (one of Shanks men). Soon, the **Black Cat pirates** arrived, leaded by Captain **Kuro**, a very fast man. His plan was to take the fortune from the richest person in the town: a young girl called **Kaya**. Luffy, Zoro, Nami, and Usopp joined forces to stop them before they could reach the town, and defeated them. As a thank you, Kaya gave them a caravel called **Going Merry**. Usopp joined Luffy’s crew as their cannon sniper.

## The Straw Hat pirates

(chapters 18-44)

Nami guided them to the **Treasure island**, but they found no treasure, just a bunch of empty chests. They also met the only human living in the island: **Gaimon**, a former pirate.

In the sea, they met the **Pirate Bounty Hunter Duo** (**Johnny** & **Yosaku**), former colleages of Zoro, who joined the crew. Luffy wanted to recruit a cook, so the pirates took them to **Baratie**, a restaurant ship leaded by **Red Food Zeff**, a former pirate that in the past spent one year in the Grand Line.

**Commodore Don Krieg**, the East Blue ruler, leader of a fleet of 50 ships and 5000 men, tried to reach the Grand Line but he met **Hawk-eye** (Dracule Mihawk), a sichibukai and the world’s best swordman, who destroyed Krieg’s fleet alone. 

Zoro, who’s ambition was to be the strongest swordman, challenged Hawk-eye to a duel, but Zoro was easily defeated. Hawk-eye revealed that the Grand Line was crawling with powerful people, and that the East Blue was the weakest sea. Then, he abandoned the place. Meanwhile, Nami escaped alone with the Going Merry towards her home island, **Cocoyashi island**.

Krieg, having no more ships, tried to capture Baratie. It was defended by the cookers and Luffy. After a hard fight, he defeated Krieg and the pirates left the place. **Sanji**, a cook with a very strong kick, joined Luffy’s crew (he wanted to get to the All Blue). As thanks, they gave Luffy another ship, and they left in the same direction as Nami. Zeff gave him his logbook containing a record of his stance at the Grand Line.

Fishmen are evolved humans that can breathe underwater and that are usually 10 times stronger than normal humans. **Arlong** was a fishman pirate right on par with Jimbei (a fishman sichibukai) that used to rampage throughout the Grand Line, and that rules over 20 towns in the East Blue. Nami used to live in Cocoyashi village with her stepmother and stepsister (**Nojiko**). Eight years ago, Arlong captured the village and killed her stepmother for not being able to pay taxes to him. Nami agreed with Arlong on buying Cocoyashi from him for 100 million berries, but meanwhile she had to join Arlong pirates as their navigator.

Now Nami had gathered 93 million berries, which hid underground close to his house at Cocoyashi. However, Arlong was secretly working with some members of the Navy and told them that Nami had a hidden treasure, so they showed up at her house and took her treasure by force (Arlong had granted them a 30%). Then, Luffy and his crew decided to fight Arlong. It was a hard fight, but Luffy finally defeated him. Then, Zoro easily defeated the Navy members allied with Arlong. The stolen treasure was recovered and Nami gave it to her village. Johnny and Yosaku decided to stay at the village, but Nami joined Luffy’s crew again and they sailed towards the Grand Line as **the Straw Hat pirates**.

Luffy’s actions so far had been noticed by the Navy, and it put a bounty on him (30 million Berries). He was starting to be famous.

Characters description:

- Luffy: Strong and skillful fighter. Childish. Wants to become the King of Pirates.
- Zoro: Strong and skillful swordman. He has poor sense of direction. Wants to become the best swordman in the world.
- Nami: Great navigator. Money is her weakness. Wants to draw the a complete map of the World.
- Usopp: Great sniper/shooter. He's a liar. Wants to become a great warrior of the sea.
- Sanji: Strong and skillful kicker, and a great cook. Women are his weakness. Wants to get to the All Blue (it has all fishes from all oceans).

## Near the Grand Line

(chapters 45-63)

Luffy decided to visit **Loguetown**, the closest city to the Grand Line, where Gold Roger was born and executed. **Captain Smoker**, a Navy captain with Devil’s fruit powers (plum/smoke fruit), was in charge of the city.

Buggy and Alvida, who joined forces and were looking for Luffy, found him at the city and tried to get their revenge. This time, Alvida had eaten a Devil’s fruit (smooth fruit), which made her skin highly slippery, making her difficult to hurt. Captain Smoker also tried to defeat Luffy. However, a man called **Dragon** saved him and let him escape from the island. Luffy and his crew sailed towards the Grand Line.

In the sea, they found a little girl (**Apis**, from **Warship island**) in a boat, who recently escaped from commodore **Nelson Royal**. She was his prisioner because she knew about dragonite, the elixir of immortality (made up from the bones of the old Millennial Dragons that used to live at **Lost island**). Luffy took her to Warship island where he discovered that a lost dragon was living there and that Apis was secretly taking care of him. Furthermore, Apis had the whisper fruit power, which made she capable of reading animal’s minds.

Some Navy troops disembarked looking for the dragon, but Luffy and his crew managed to take it to their ship and escaped towards Lost island. They found an abandoned island with ruins and paintings revealing that Warship island was Lost island. They headed back to Warship island, but Nelson’s fleet surrounded and attacked them. They also fought **Eric**, a mercenary with the Sickle fruit powers hired by Nelson to get the dragon. During the battle, the surroundings of the island emerged, and a flock of dragons arrived. This event happens every 1000 years, and dragons migrate to it when that happens. The dragons attacked the enemy fleet, and Luffy destroyed the flagship. Unfortunately, their dragon friend died during the Navy’s attack.

Apis decided to stay in Lost island, and the Straw Hat pirates continued their travel towards the Grand Line. They got to Reverse mountain and accessed through one its channels to the Grand Line.

<br>![map_2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AnselmoGPP/know_base/master/topics/miscellany/resources/one_piece_3.png)

At the entrance to the Grand Line they found a lighthouse, and became friends of the lighthouse keeper and his giant whale (biggest whale of the world). The islands at the Grand Line have magnetic minerals that prevent compasses from working. The keeper gave them a **log pose**, a special compass that points a way through some islands along the Grand Line (compasses doesn't work in the Grand Line due to the magnetism between islands). At the end of this path of islands lies **Raftel island**, only confirmed by Gold Roger, and where it’s thought his treasure lies, although nobody has reached it. To be able to follow the path of islands, the log pose has to stay some time in each island in order to get enough magnetism to point to the next island in the route.

## Into the Grand Line

(chapters 64-91)

Straw Hat pirates used the log pose to navigate through the Grand Line. The log pose took them to an island with huge cacti and the **Whiskey Peak town**. However, this place was one of the bases of **Baroque Works**, a criminal syndicate, which tried to capture them to get Luffy’s bounty. However, Zoro and Luffy easily stopped them. They defeated two enemies with Devil’s fruit powers: **Mr. 5** (Bomb fruit) and **Miss Valentine** (Kilo fruit). **Nefertari Vivi**, princess of **Arabasta Kingdom**, was infiltrated in Baroque Works ranks because they were spreading civil war in her kingdom. However, she was discovered, but Straw Hat pirates saved her. She wanted to go back home, so she and his big duck, **Karoo**, joined them. She told them that Baroque Works’ leader was **Crocodile** (**Mr. 0**), the 7th warlord of the sea.

Meanwhile, Coby and his friend **Helmeppo** (son of the former captain Morgan), who were working as chores boys for the Navy, were taken by **Vice-Admiral Garp** to the **Navy-Headquarters**, at the Grand Line, where they started training hard.

Straw Hat pirates arrived to **Little Garden**. They became friends of the only two people living in the island: **Broggy** and **Dorry**, two giants from the country of **Elbaf** that had been living in the island for 100 years. Soon, some members of Baroque Works arrived: **Mr. 3** (Wax fruit), **Miss Golden Week** (hipnotizing powers), Mr. 5, and Miss Valentine. They tried to kill Straw Hat pirates and capture the giants (there was an old bounty of 100 million berries on each one because they were leaders of the **Giant Warriors pirates**). Their next step was to attack Arabasta kingdom. However, they were defeated, and Sanji could steal from them an **Eternal log** (type of log pose that permanently points to a specific island and needs no time to recharge magnetism), which pointed to Arabasta kingdom.

Straw hat pirates continued their journey. Nami got sick and had a fever. They were attacked by **Wapol** (Munch fruit) and his pirates, but they defeated him easily. Then, they arrived to **Big Horn village**, in the snowy **Drum island**, where **Dalton** (Ox fruit), the captain of the security squad of the island, kindly offered his house to them. Dalton explained that the island was a country called **Drum kingdom**, governed by king Wapol, until a few months ago a small group of 5 powerful pirates leaded by **Blackbeard** (Marshall D. Teach) arrived to the island and destroyed the country. Wapol escaped to the sea (where Straw Hat pirates met him). There was only one doctor in the island, **Dr. Kureha** (139 years old), which was living at the top of 5 kms height mountain, in Wapol’s castle, so Luffy and Sanji took Nami there. In their way, they had to fight some dangerous rabbits (**Lapins**) and escaped from an avalanche caused by them. Kureha cured Nami. She was ill because a bacteria (**Kestia**) infected her at Little Garden.

Kureha was living with **Chopper**, a reindeer with human abilities (speak and become human-like) since he ate the Human fruit. Soon, Wapol and his army arrived to the island. He was an awful king, so Dalton and Big Horn’s people tried to stop him without success, and Dalton almost die. Then, Wapol arrived to his former castle and Luffy and Chopper fought and defeated him. Chopper joined them, and the Straw Hat pirates continued their journey.

Characters description:

- Chopper: Great doctor. Compliments are his weakness. Wants to become a doctor that can cure any disease in the World.

## Arabasta kingdom

(chapters 92-130)

Arabasta kingdom, at **Sandy island**, was in the middle of a civil war. The royal army was losing the fight against the **Resistance**. Crocodile was considered a hero in Arabasta, even by king **Nefertari Cobra** (Vivi’s father), because he helped to get rid of pirates. But he was evil inside, he was secretly driving the civil war with the purpose of taking down the kingdom. His organization, Baroque Works, had 2000 men.

Meanwhile, Ace (flame fruit), sworn brother of Luffy and now commander of the 2nd division of **Whitebeard’s pirates** (Whitebeard, or Edward Newgate, is considered the strongest man in the world), was in a search and kill mission of Blackbeard, a former subordinate of him who betrayed and killed some Whitebeard’s pirates.

The Straw Hat pirates finally arrived to Arabasta, to **Nanohana** city, where they got supplies. Vivi’s plan was to meet the rebel’s leader, **Kohza**, a childhood friend, and convince him to stop fighting the king and defeat Crocodile instead. The kingdom was suffering a severe drought for 3 years. Some Navy troops leaded by Captain Smoker, and major sergeant **Tashigi**, were at Nanohana looking for the Straw Hat pirates. They almost capture the pirates, but Ace saved them. Vivi sent Karoo with a letter for the king revealing him that Crocodile was the real enemy. Then they left Nanohana and navigated towards **Erumalu** city. On their way, Ace destroyed a Baroque Works fleet that tried to attack them. Erumalu turned out to be abandoned due to the drought. After finding out that Blackbeard was not in Arabasta, Ace left the group to continue his search in the west.

They continued through the desert towards **Yuba oasis**, where they thought the rebel leader was, but once there they discovered that the rebels had moved to **Karotea** city, so they decided to continue towards **Rainbase** city, where Crocodile was, to attack him. However, once in Rainbase, the pirates, Vivi and Smoker were captured by Crocodile (sand fruit), except for Sanji and Chopper.

Crocodile secretly expected the kingdom to collapse so he could take control of it. He had caused the drought with his sand powers. And with lies and malicious actions he had decreased the popularity of the king. Ultimately, he kidnapped the king, provided weapons to the rebels, and induced them into attacking **Alubarna**, the capital city and the king's home.

Sanji rescued the prisioners while Chopper was performing a distraction. The pirates and Vivi went to Alubarna to stop the clash between the rebels and the royal army, but Luffy was forced to stay and fight Crocodile.

Luffy was defeated, but survived. In Alubarna, the pirates and Vivi tried to stop the clash, but failed, and the battle started in the city. Crocodile arrive to Alubarna, planning to kill both armies by bombarding the city. Kohza discovered Crocodile's plan and that he was who provoked Arabasta ruin. He tried to stop the battle, but Baroque Works had members infiltrated in both sides that prevented it by causing confusion and fear.

Luffy arrived to Alubarna and fought Crocodile. Luffy used water to counter Crocodile's sand powers, but was defeated again. Crocodile was secretely looking for Pluton, the "most feared military power of the world", which was hidden somewhere in Arabasta. The Marines joined the fight to help the pirates save the city. Vivi and the pirates found Crocodile's bomb an **Pell**, an officer of the king with the ability to fly, sacrificed himself by taking it to the sky right before it exploded, saving the city. However, Pell survived.

Luffy fights Crocodile again, defeating him this time. The rain returns to Arabasta. The battle stopped and everybody realized that Crocodile was the real enemy. The marines captured Crocodile and their men (Baroque Works) and took them to their headquarters. Peace returns to the kingdom.

A bounty is put on Zoro (60 million), and Luffy's bounty increases (100 million). The pirates leave the island but the marines, leaded by captain **Hina** (cage fruit), try to capture them in the sea. They escape with the help of **Mr. 2**, or **Bon Clay** (copy fruit), an ex-Baroque Works member that became their friend.

**Nico Robin** (flower fruit), an ex-Baroque Works member that saved Luffy twice from dying when fighting Crocodile, joins the pirates. She's an archeologist looking for the **River Poneglyph** (true text of history). In Arabasta she found a Poneglyph (unbreakable stone with an old language text) detailing where Pluton was, but she didn't tell Crocodile about this.

Characters description:

- Robin (flower fruit): Archeologist and good fighter. No clear weakness. Wants to uncover the true history of the world, specifically the Void Century, by deciphering the River Poneglyph.

## The Rainbow mist

(chapters 131-143)

Next events:

- The pirates arrived to an uninhabited island, gathered resources, and continued their journey.
- They arrived to an island where they got supplies at a town and witnessed local fireworks.
- They arrived to a small island and became friends with an old castaway who lived there alone with the island's goats. The marines attacked the pirates in the island, but they escaped to the sea.
- The marines leaded by Major **Pasqua** found them, but the pirates managed to escape.

They pirates arrived to an island where they visited the town of **Ruluka**. They met scientist **Henzo**, who was studying the **Rainbow mist**, which coincidentally appeared near the island shortly after. The pirates and Henzo went to explore it. The mist took them to another place (called Ape's Concert), outside regular time and space. Time there flows much slower, and escaping is difficult. There were many ships with treasures (ship graveyard) and a few kids trapped there. The kids were old friends of Henzo that got inside the mist 50 years ago and didn't grow up. Ruluka's major, **Whetton**, entered the mist with his men to get its treasures. Whetton was a pirate that attacked Ruluka 50 years ago but, after losing his ship in the rainbow mist, he became Ruluka's major. Finally, everybody escaped from the mist and Whetton was captured by some marines for trying to keep the treasures instead of giving them to the World Government. These marines turned out to be the Henzo's friends, who returned from the mist to 50 years ago and became marines. 

## The sky island

(chapters 144-195)

In the sea, the log pose points to the sky. Robin tells that she read in a book that there's an island in the sky, though it's not visible. Shortly after, a big ship falls from the sky. It's the Sent Blis (or Saint Bliss), a 200 yo ship, broken and with corpses. From it, the pirates get a map of **Skypiea**, a land of the sky. They also meet **Masira** and his pirates, who came there to get the old ship. However, the day became dark all of a sudden and they saw the silhouette of huge unknown monsters, so both pirate groups escaped.

Using an eternal log that Robin stole from Masira, the pirates get to **Mocktown**, in **Jaya** island, an island of pirates, looking for a way to get to the island in the sky. However, nobody there believed such island exists. Pirate **Bellamy** (spring fruit) explains that there're many currents, and one of them is the **Knock Up stream** (huge water jet caused by underground geotermal activity) which takes ships to the sky to make them fall later. This made some people think that there was an island in the sky. However, the ex-pirate **Montblanc Cricket**, friend of Masira and his brother **Soho**, tells them about the Cumuloregalis, a especial type of cloud that doesn't let light go through and keeps the same shape for millenia. So, if the island exists, that's the only place where it could be.

Cricket is descendant of the explorer **Montblanc Norland**, who 400 year ago said that he had discovered a city of gold in Jaya island. The king travelled with Norland to see that city, but it had dissappeared. Norland was regarded as a liar and executed by the king. Cricket use to dive near the island trying to find the city underwater.

With the help of Cricket, Masira, Soho, and their pirates, the straw hat pirates got into the Knock Up stream and reached a sea in the sky: the White Sea, which is 7.000 meters above sea level. They met **Gan Fall**, the Sky's knight, who rides a bird that can transform into a Pegaso (this bird ate the horse fruit). He told them that 10.000 meters above sea level was the White-White Sea. Later, they found a gate that took them up there and arrived to Skypiea, which is composed of a few islands.

In **Angel island** they met a girl called **Conis** and his father, **Pagaya**. They explained many things about Skypiea. There're two types of cloud there: marine clouds (similar to liquid) and island clouds (more solid). The sky's people make great use of **dials** (seashells from the sky with different properties like sound recording, propulsion, heat...). They let Nami drive a **waver** (single-person vehicle powered by a dial).

**God Enel** (lightning fruit) governs Skypiea ("God" is the title of the governor of Skypiea). In Skypiea there're two islands: Angel island, made of clouds and inhabited by the sky people, and **Upper Yard**, a sacred and forbidden island made of actual ground and inhabited by Enel, his four **Priests** (**Satori**, **Shura**, **Gedatsu**, **Ohm**), and his 50 god's warriors. Using the mantra ability, Enel and the priests can predict the moves of their opponents and feel their presence.

Since the pirates were illegaly in Skypiea, the **White Berets**, a police body controlled by the Priests, tried to capture them, but they were defeated. However, the Going Merry with Nami, Robin, Zoro, and Chopper was captured and taken to the sacrificial altar in the **Lost Forest** of Upper Yard for the priests to sacrifice them. Luffy, Usopp, and Sanji went to Upper Yard to rescue them, and defeated Satori. Exploring the forest, Nami discovered that Upper Yard was a piece of the Jaya island that was taken up by the Knock Up stream about 400 year ago. Gan Fall joined the pirates to help them.

The **Shandians** (people of Shandia) are people that live in a village hidden in the clouds and are at war with Angel island and Upper Yard. They took advantage of the moment and attacked Upper Yard leaded by **Wiper**, but they were defeated by Ohm.

Meanwhile, all the Straw Hat pirates managed to get together and decided look in the island for the old abandoned city of **Shandora**, which was made of gold according to a legend. Gan Fall explained that 400 years ago, the sky's people were living normally until the Upper Yard appeared. The sky's people took control of the island, expelling the original population, the Shandians. Gan Fall was god of Skypiea until 6 year ago, until Enel and his priests arrived from another island of the sky and took control of Skypiea. That's wy Shandians are at war with the sky's people and god.

The Shandians attacked again, this time defeating Shura. Enel sent his 50 god's warriors to stop them, and a battle began where both sides suffered many casualties. Enel joined the fight and burned down Usopp and Sanji. Robin defeated the god's warriors chief. Luffy was swallowed by a giant snake. Chopper defeated Gedatsu, but he was later defeated by Ohm, who in turn was defeated by Zoro. After all god's warriors and priests were defeated, the last ones standing were Zoro, Robin, Nami, Wiper, and Gan Fall. They fought Enel, but they were defeated since Enel was made of electricity.

Enel's secret plan was to destroy Skypiea, since it considers its existence unnatural, and return to his place of birth, the **Fairy Vearth**, another sky island (although some people say he was born in Bilka, an sky island that he destroyed), using a flying ship that he forced his prisioners to build. This ship flies using Enel's electricity and carries the gold of Shandora.

Luffy escaped from the snake's stomach and fought Enel on his flying ship. Since Luffy is made of rubber, electricity doesn't affects him, so he is immune to Enel's electric attacks. However, Luffy was defeated and thrown overboard. Enel began destroying Skypiea throwing lightning bolts from the sky. Angel island was wiped out. Luffy managed to get to the ship again, this time defeating Enel.

Luffy rang the golden bell of Shandora, which was heard far away by the people of Jaya, including Cricket, realizing that Norland was not a liar. Enel escaped from Skypiea towards Fairy Vearth (the Moon). The sky people and the Shandians became friends and lived peacefully in Upper Yard, since Angel island dissappeared.

Robin found a Poneglyph under the golden bell detailing the location of an ancient weapon called Poseidon. Golden Roger was there and left a note, making Robin realize that the River Poneglyph is a collective message formed by assembling different poneglyphs scattered across the world. All them together form a path to Raftel. Gan Fall confirmed that Golden Roger was there 20 years ago.

The pirates took as much gold as they could. Usopp also took some dials (impact, breath, flame...), and Nami took a waver. Then, they went to the Cloud End and left Skypiea using a Balloon Octopus.

## Navarone

(chapters 196-206)

The pirates landed in **Navarone** (in an inner lake), a Navy's marine fortress run by **Commander Jonathan** and containing more than 1000 marines. The pirates left the ship and infiltrated the base right before the marines boarded and captured the ship. Zoro and Usopp were captured, but Sanji and Luffy rescued them from the prison. They overcame a few ambushes, recovered the Going Merry, and almost escape.

However, they decided to go back to recover their gold (confiscated by the marines) and the waver (forgotten by Nami). They hide the ship and disguised it as a Navy's one. Luffy, Zoro, Usopp, and Robin went for the gold. Nami and Sanji went for the waver. They got both after overcoming a couple of traps, got to the ship and tried to escape. The marines reduced the water in the lake, and the ship grounded and got stucked, but the pirates escaped flying using a breath dial and flame dial to inflate the balloon octopus with hot air, and an impact dial for propelling. Once safe, they returned the octopus to the sea.

## Long Ring Long Land

(chapters 207-219)

The pirates arrived to the island Long Ring Long Land, which is like a ring. Most of the time the sea level covers parts of the island, so it becomes 10 different islands, but once a year the sea level decreases and for a short time the island becomes a ring. In a wide meadow, the pirates saw that animals and trees were all elongated. They met Tonjit, an old man belonging to a group of nomads that move around the ring, moving to the next island every 3 years. He had been alone for 10 years since he lost his people (because he got on stilts but then was too afraid to get off and nobody knew where he was).

The **Foxy** pirates arrived to the island too and their captain, Foxy (slow fruit), challenged Luffy to a **Davy Back Fight**, a pirate game where cheats are allowed used for getting members of other pirate crews. He accepted, so they played 3 rounds of games:

- Boat race around the island: Foxy pirates win because Foxy used his power to slow down his rivals. They take Chopper.
- Ball game: Straw Hat pirates win. They take Chopper back.
- Boxing match (Luffy Vs. Foxy): Luffy won.

The Foxy pirates left the island. Tonjit's nephew, who has become an excavator, shows up in the island. He was looking for his granfather, and finally found him. The Straw Hat pirates left the island.

## Stolen memories

(chapters 220-224)

The pirates arrived to an island. They planned to sleep in the ship and disembark in the morning. However, when they woke up in the morning, they had lost their recent memory. Nobody remembered his friends, except for Robin, who was reading the whole night and didn't sleep. Their memories were stolen by a kid during the night using a special flute. The next night the kid came again to steal more memories, but the pirates were alert and, when they heard the flute, they attacked the kid. Luffy recovered his memories from the flute, but the kid escaped.

In the island they discovered a town where nobody had memories at all. The kid hipnotized Zoro and made him attack Luffy. Meanwhile, Sanji attacked the kid while he was trying to hipnotize the others. They discovered that the kid was also hipnotized and that the real enemy was the flute itself, which was a seahorse monster. The pirates fought the seahorse, making him vomit the stolen memories. Luffy hit it him so hard that sent him flying very far away. Everybody in the island recovered their memories.

## Admiral Aokiji

(chapters 225-228)

During a storm, the pirates rescued Foxy and two subordinates, and later helped him recover their crew (500 men) from Kiba Kaeru pirates, who had captured them. Despite all this, the treacherous Foxy tried to capture the Straw Hat pirates to get their gold and collect the rewards for their capture. However, he was defeated.

The Straw Hat pirates arrived to an island where they gathered food. The Foxy pirates attacked them again, but Luffy defeated them easily. Inside the island they met **Admiral Aokiji** (Ice fruit), also called **Kuzan**, admiral of the Marine's headquarters and one of the most poweful men of the government. He was there just to confirm that Robin was with the Straw Hat pirates.

Robin explained that there're only 3 admirals: **Akainu**, **Aokiji**, and **Kizaru**. They receive orders from General Admiral **Sengoku**, who is one of the three combat forces of the government.

There was a group of castaways in the island whose ship was destroyed by **Yokozuna**, a huge frog. Aokiji used his power to froze a part of the sea, so the castaways could walk to another island.

Aokiji revealed that he knew Luffy's grandfather, and that Robin got a bounty of 79 million at 8 yo. He tried to capture Robin in the past, but failed. He tried again, but the Straw Hat pirates fought him to save her. Aokiji defeated them, but spared Luffy's life because he was grateful for defeating Crocodile. Then, he left the island on his bike. The pirates continued their journey.

## Water 7

(chapters 229-263)

In the sea, the pirates saw Yokozuna. Following it they arrived to a train station with a lighthouse in the middle of the sea. They saw a Umi Ressha train (Puffing Tom) going over the water, running on submarine railroad tracks, taking passengers from one island to another. This train connected different cities in different islands: Water 7 (city of water), Pucchi (city of the gourmet), ST Poplar (city of Spring queen), and San Faldo (city of carnival), and Enies Lobby (judicial island, government stronghold).

The pirates arrived to **Water 7**, a half submerged city. They exchanged their gold for money (getting 300 million). Then, they visited the shipyards (the bests of the world), where they met **Iceburg**, city mayor, president of Galley-La Company (company of shipwrights), and world's best shipwright. Shipwright **Kaku** inspected the ship and concluded that it was damaged beyond repair. Meanwhile, Robin disappeared in the city.

**Family Franky**, a group of dismantlers and bounty hunters, stole 200 million from Usopp. This made Usopp feel useless and regret. The pirates tried to recover it by attacking Franky's base, but **Franky** had already left the city with the money. Luffy decided to replace the Going Merry with another ship, but Usopp strongly disagreed and abandoned the Straw Hat pirates.

In the morning, it was discovered that Iceburg was victim of an assasination attempt at his mansion. He survived to 5 shots and identified the attackers: a masked man and Nico Robin. Meanwhile, Franky returned to Water 7 after spending all the money elsewhere, and attacked Luffy in the city after realizing that the Straw Hat pirates destroyed his base. Franky turned out to be a cyborg. Soon, five strong Galley's shipwrights arrived and attacked Luffy too, accusing the Straw Hat pirates of the assasination attempt, but Luffy escaped. The whole city began to look for the pirates.

Sanji and Chopper found Robin in the city, but she told them that she was leaving the Straw Hat pirates. Actually, she had joined the secret organization **CP9** (Cipher Pol Number 9). At night, Robin and four masked people attacked the mansion again. The Straw Hat pirates expected that, and were waiting for an opportunity to get to Robin.

The study and deciphering of ponyglyphs is forbidden by the World Government because they tell where the **Ancient Weapons** are. The only person in the world able to decipher them is Robin, that's why a bounty was put on her at 8 yo. Iceburg has the blueprints of one of these weapons: **Pluton**, a warship built long ago at Water 7.

The government has 8 secret services (CP1 to CP8) located at different places arount the world. CP9 is similar, but with more privileges, like licence to kill. The governemnt sent CP9 to steal from Iceburg the blueprints of an ancient weapon called **Pluton** (a powerful warship built long ago at Water 7). The government planed to revive the Ancient Weapons in order to end the Pirate Era. The four masked attackers were very strong governemnt agents who infiltrated Water 7 five years ago and became subordinates of Iceburg: **Rucchi** (shipwright, Leopard fruit), **Kaku** (shipwright), **Califa** (secretary), and **Blueno** (bartender, Door fruit). CP9 asked Robin for help, and she joined them to protect the Straw Hat pirates from CP9 making the **Buster Call** (call for a powerful force of 5 viceadmirals and 10 warships).

Iceburg and Franky were apprentices of **Tom**, world's best shipwright, president of Tom's Workers, and inventor of Umi Ressha. Tom taught them the trade and gave Iceburg the blueprints. CP5, leaded by commander Spandam, tried to get the blueprints from Tom, but failed. The government accused Tom of building the **Gold Jackson**, Gold D. Roger's ship, and sent him to Enies Lobby.

CP9 interrogated Iceburg in his bedroom and discovered that he didn't have the blueprints because long ago he gave them to Franky. Suddenly, the Straw Hat pirates together with shipwright **Pauly** attacked them. However, they were defeated, and CP9 set the building on fire. Fortunately, Chopper saved them from the fire.

CP9 visited Franky and took him prisioner, together with Usopp. To save her friends from the Buster Call, Robin agreed to be taken prisioner too, since the government wanted her to revive the Ancient Weapons. After this, the CP9, the prisioners, and many government agents and marines took the Puffing Tom towards Enies Lobby. The train departed, but Sanji, who was close by, jumped on the train. The Straw Hat pirates, three shipwrights (Pauly, Tilestone, Lulu), and the Franky Family departed towards Enies Lobby using a Umi Ressha prototype (Rocket Man). Sanji was only able to extract Usopp, and both were rescued by the Rocket Man.

## Enies Lobby

(chapters 264-312)

Enies Lobby is a World Government stronghold located on the **Never-Night island** (it always has daylight). It's a judicial island surrounded by a huge waterfall, and populated by marines, agents, and the CP9 members. The key buildings are: two consecutive main gates, the courtyard, the courthouse, the CP9's headquarters (Tower of Justice), and the Bridge of Hesitation. The courtyard and the Tower of Justice are connected by a drawbridge over the waterfall. The Tower of Justice and the Bridge of Hesitation are separated by a big piece of sea. Right in front of the bridge are the **Gates of Justice**, which take to **Impel Down** (submarine prison) and **Marineford** (Marine's headquarters).

The CP9 members Rucchi, Kaku, Califa, and Blueno joined the other CP9 members at the Tower of Justice: **Fukurou** (fat), **Kumadori** (honor), and **Jabra** (Wolf fruit). Their commander, Spandam (weak), former CP5 commander, gave Devil's fruits to Kaku (Jiraffe fruit) and Califa (Bubble fruit) to make them stronger.

Soon, the Straw Hat pirates and their new friends arrived with the Rocket Man and assaulted Enies Lobby, fighting their way through the island towards the Tower of Justice. Luffy went ahead alone and defeated Blueno at the top of the courthouse. Franky burned down Pluton blueprints to prevent them from being seized by the government. Since the bridge between the courtyard and the Tower was broken, the pirates and Franky used the Rocket Man to jump over the waterfall and reach the Tower of Justice. There, Franky defeated Fukurou, and Chopper defeated Kumadori.

Meanwhile, Spandam and Rucchi took Robin through an underground passage under the sea connecting the Tower of Justice with the Bridge of Hesitation. Luffy reached them in at the end of the passage, in a pillar of the bridge. Rucchi stayed to fight him while Spandam continued his way with Robin. Soon, Spandam activated the Buster Call. The Gates of Justice were opened in order to transfer Robin to a Marine's ship on the other side of the Bridge of Hesitation (to take her to Impel Down), and to let the Buster Call's warships reach Enies Lobby.

At the Tower of Justice, Nami defeated Califa, Sanji defeated Jabra, and Zoro defeated Kaku. Franky reached Spandam and Robin at the Bridge of Hesitation, while sniper Usopp attacked Spandam and his marines from the Tower of Justice. Franky liberated Robin, and both captured the Marine's ship. Meanwhile, the warships began bombarding Enies Lobby.

The fight between Luffy and Rucchi moved to the first pillar of the Bridge of Hesitation. All the other pirates and Franky gathered on top of the bridge. Sanji went to activate the gates closure. The warships disembarked hundreds of marines on the bridge to recapture Robin, and a fight started. Luffy defeated Rucchi after a very hard fight. The warships began bombarding the bridge. Unexpectedly, the Going Merry appeared next to the bridge (the ship itself had come for the rescue), so the pirates jumped onto it. The gates closure created whirlpools that reduced warships' maneuverability. Zoro, Sanji, and Luffy deviated many cannon attacks, Robin killed Spandam from a distance, Franky used a wind-attack to move the ship away from the fleet, and Usopp created a smokescreen. Meanwhile, the other attackers (Franky family and shipwrights) left Enies Lobby taking the train to Water 7.

While navigating towards Water 7, the Going Merry got definetely broken. The pirates got rescued by Iceburg with a Galley-La Company ship, and they burned down the Going Merry before returning to Water 7.

Robin background:

The **Empty Century** is a century whose history is not known. It's history is written in the poneglyphs, but the government forbade investigating them to prevent anybody from reviving the Ancient Weapons. Robin used to live in the **O'hara** island, where she became an archeologist. The island had the biggest library in the world. In the island there was a group of scholars, including Robin's mother, that secretely studied the poneglyphs. According to their research, they considered that in the past there was a great kingdom, which somehow disappeared during the Empty Century, replaced by the World Government. They think that the World Government attacked them, so they left the poneglyphs (unbreakable stones with a message) so people from the future could know the truth. The scholars were discovered by the government, so they made the Buster Call and destroyed the whole island with its population. The only survivor was Robin. Vice-admiral Kuzen took pity on her and secretely let her escape in a boat. Since Robin knew how to read the poneglyphs, the government put a bounty on her (79 million).

## Towards the New World

(chapters 313-335)

Soon, viceadmiral **Monkey D. Garp**, Luffy's grandfather, arrived to Water 7. He revealed interesting information:

- Pirate Shanks (Luffy's old friend) and Whitebeard are two of the 4 emperors that rule the second part of the Grand Line. The only people capable to face them are the Marine and the Sichibukai.
- The man called Dragon that saved Luffy's life at Loguetown was actually Luffy's father, the revolutionary **Monkey D. Dragon**, who leads the Revolutionary Army, a group that opposes the government. The government named him the worst criminal in the world. Luffy didn't know who his father was.

Under Garp's command was Sergeant Major Coby (Luffy's old friend) and Sergeant Helmeppo (Zoro's and Luffy's old enemy). Coby revealed information:

- The Marine's ships can navigate through the Calm Belt while avoiding sea monsters because their ships are covered with seastone, which emits sea-like energy, making them undetectable.
- Dr. Vegapunk, the most important scientist of the Marine, is studying how Devil's fruit powers are transferred, and developed a method for making inanimate objects eat a Devil's fruit.
- The second part of the Grand Line (on the other side of the Red Line) is called the **New World**.

After visiting Luffy, Garp decided not to try to capture the pirates, and the marines left Water 7.

A World Government's fleet sent to the New World was defeated. Shanks met Whitebeard to talk.

Shanks and Buggy (the Clown) used to work for Gold D. Roger. After Roger's death, both crewmates got separated.

The World Governemnt updated bounties: Luffy (300 mill.), Zoro (120 mill.), Robin (80 mill.), Sanji (77 mill.), Franky (44 mill.), Nami (16 mill.), Chopper (50). After Enies Lobby's incident, the Straw Hat pirates became famous around the world.

The pirates stayed at Water 7 for a while. Meanwhile, Iceburg designed a new brigantine, and built it together with Franky, Pauly, Tilestone, and Lulu. It was made of **Adam wood** (strongest and highest-quality wood). It was called the **Thousand Sunny**, and was given to the Straw Hat pirates. It was much bigger than the Going Merry.

After a Luffy's invitation, Franky joined the pirates as shipwright. Ussop was accepted back onto the crew after he apologized. The pirates left Water 7. On the sea, Garp attacked them. The General Admiral Sengoku got angry because Garp didn't capture the pirates before, so Garp was sent again to capture them. However, the pirates escaped using a propulsion system of the ship that uses soda to jump 1 km away.

Meanwhile, Ace found Blackbeard (darkness fruit) at **Banaro island**, which he was attacking with his 4 pirates. After Blackbeard destroyed the town, both fought.

Blackbeard used to work for Whitebeard, but his purpose was to get the darkness fruit. When Whitebeard's 4th division commander, Sacchi, found the fruit, he killed him, took the fruit, and left Whitebeard's pirates. Ace, the 2nd division commander, was sent to find and kill Blackbeard.

The Phoenix pirates, leaded by captain **Puzzle Phoenix** (bounty: 100 mill.), got into the New World, but they soon realized that pirates there were too strong for them, so they fled back from there. However, they were captured by the **Accino family**, a bounty-hunting clan, and forced to help them capture pirates. The Accino family had 7 members and some employees: Family hunters and Domo penguins, and now the Phoenix pirates too. Their leader is **Don Accino** (hot fruit). Their domain is **Kyokaido**, an island covered with ice and surrounded with icebergs. Their base is **Lovely Land**, deep within Kyokaido.

The Straw Hat pirates found a drifting ship, so they decided to help its crew. However, they were the Phoenix pirates in disguise. They tried to poison and capture de SH pirates, but failed. The SHPs entered **Kyokaido**, where the Acchino family tried to capture them. They stole the SHPs' flag and captured Usopp, Sanji, Nami, Zoro, and Franky, who were taken to Lovely Land. Robin helped them escape, the SHPs joined the Phoenix pirates, and both attacked Lovely Land to recover their stolen flags.

The Accino family was defeated and the flags recovered. The fight between Don Accino and Luffy activated the volcano under the island and it destroyed the whole island.

## Into the New World

(chapters 337-)

The pirates continued their journey and entered into the **Florian Triangle**, a dark sea covered with mist and full of storms. There, they found a ship and befriended its single crew member: a living skeleton called **Brook** (rebirth fruit). He was a pirate that died decades ago when other pirates attacked his ship and killed the crew. However, the Rebirth fruit took effect and, after some time, he came back to life as a skeleton.

The SHPs and Brook were found by **Thriller Bark**, a ghost island that moves around the sea using huge sails. While exploring the island, they were attacked by zombies. Some of them were zombie generals, which were especially strong zombies.

In the island was living the sichibukai **Gecko Moria** (shadow fruit). He has the power to take the shadow (related to the soul) of an individual, and introduce it into a dead body, reviving it as a zombie. Such zombies have the skills and personality of the original shadow's owner. **Dr. Hogback** helped Moria get dead bodies, and even create new types of zombies by combining human and animal bodies. Moria is always trying to capture pirates to increase his army of zombies.

Sanji, Zoro, and Luffy were captured, and Moria used their shadows to revive three zombies that were used later to attack the pirates. Franky and Robin were attacked by a giant spider, but Brook easily killed it. Brook had been in the island more than 5 years ago, where he got his shadow stolen.

## Alternative sagas

There're a number of alternative stories inserted here and there in the main anime series:

- **Grand Jipangu**: Story set in a feudal japan-inspired country.
- **Chopper man**: Story about the antagonism between a hero (Chopper man) and a villain (Dr. Usodabada).

## Grand Jipangu

Story set in an alternate reality, in a feudal japan-inspired country called **Grand Jipangu**. Main characters:

- **Luffy** (also, **Straw Hat**): Okkapiki (secret police from Edo period, detective) in the town of Kazaguruma.
- **Sanji** and **Onami** (Nami) run a restaurant
- **Usopp**: Subordinate of Luffy
- Sensei (doctor) **Blue Nose** (Chopper)
- **Bou** (priest) (Zoro)
- **Princess** (Vivi): Shogun's daughter.
- **Tono** (lord) (Nefertari Cobra): Shogun.

(chapter 291)

The Clown family, leaded by **Buggy**, was a criminal group trying to take control of the town. The **bugyo** (magistrate, samurai official) (Mr. 3) of Kitamachi was bribed by Buggy to organize a conspiracy to get rid of Straw Hat. Buggy wanted to collect a debt from somebody called **Totsan** (meaning, father), so he captured **Orika** (friend of Luffy) to sell her as slave, thinking that she was Totsan's daughter. Straw Hat attacked him to prevent that, but the bugyo was present and considered that an unjustified attack, so he relieved him from his position (according to Buggy's and the bugyo's plan). However, Vivi, the Shogun's daughter, was present, and claimed that the bugyo was under investigation for accepting a bribe and conspiracy. The bugyo surrendered, Straw Hat expelled Buggy from the town. Later, Straw Hat, Orika, Totsan, and some friends celebrated Christmas (a foreign celebration) at the restaurant of Sanji and Onami.

(chapter 292)

In the new year, the race of Mochimaki (rice cake throwing) is celebrated at the town's castle. One mochi has inside a treasure called Kongou Seki valued at 10.000 ryou berries. All the mochis are thrown from the castle, and everybody below try to find the treasure mochi. Buggy wanted to get the treasure-mochi at all costs.

Onami, Sanji, and Sensei Blue Nose (Doctor Chopper) were carrying the mochis to the castle (Castle of Arabasta), but they got ambushed by the Clown family. They managed to run through the ambush, but shortly after they got ambushed again. This time, they were protected by Luffy, and the mochis were carried to the castle. The Clown family decided to attack the castle. They captured Vivi, trying to force his father **Tono** (lord) to hand over the treasure-mochi. At this point, Buggy changed his plan: now he wanted to kill Tono and rule the entire country. However, he was defeated, and the Mochimaki took place.

(chapter 303)

Once a year, in Spring, takes place the Hana-Mi festival (picnic under the cherry trees), where people of the town gather under a thousand-year-old cherry tree (the Sennen-Zakura). Nami's and Sanji's restaurant sold many bentou (food in a box). However, the Zakura disappeared misterously during the night. Robin discovered that the tree was stolen by pirates and hidden on a ship behind an island. The Clown family had stolen the tree for **Silver Fox** (Foxy), who wanted to sell it elsewhere. Luffy, Sanji, and Usopp attacked the pirates to recover the tree. Zoro, who was sleeping on the tree, joined the fight. The thieves were defeated, and Luffy expelled Foxy and Buggy.

## Chopper Man

Story set in an alternate and more animated reality. Main characters:

- **Chopper Man** (Chopper): Super hero that can fly.
- **Dr. Usodabada** (Usopp): Villain that wants to conquer the world.
- **Sanjirops** (Sanji): Monster subordinate of Usodabada.
- **Zorokiller** (Zoro): Swordman subordinate of Usodabada.
- **Namifia** (Nami): Secretary of Chopper Man.
- **Giant Luffy Bomber** (Luffy): Giant robot of Chopper.
- **Frandasher** (Franky): Giant robot of Usodabada.

(chapters 336)

Trying to increase the size of his organization, Usodabada occupied the television station to announce that he was hiring new personnel. To attract more attention, he organized a fight between him and Chopper. He made some tricks to win, but he was finally defeated. Usodabada called Frankdasher, his giant robot, to avenge him, but he was defeated by Chopper's giant robot, the Giant Luffy Bomber.
