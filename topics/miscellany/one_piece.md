## Introduction

In the world there are many island and only one continent, the **Red Line**, which circles the planet from north to south. The **Grand Line** is an oceanic current that goes along the ecuator. The Red Line and the Grand Line surround the **4 seas**: North Blue, South Blue, West Blue, East blue (where this story begins). The **All blue** is a mythical sea somewhere. The Grand Line is surrounded by two parallel belts where there is no wind and no current (**Calm Belt**), home of huge sea monsters (**Sea Kings**). In the Red Line there's the **Reverse mountain**, which has 5 channels that connect the Grand Line and the 4 seas at the top, and serve as a point of access to any of these regions.

<br>![map_1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AnselmoGPP/know_base/master/topics/miscellany/resources/one_piece_2.png)

The **Devil's fruit** is a rare type of fruit that gives powers to those who eat it, but take away their ability to swim. There're many types of them. The **Kairouseki** is a crystallized form of the sea that temporarilly suppresses the powers of people that have eaten a devil's fruit.

**Gold Roger**, the King of Pirates, got arrested and executed by the **World Government**. Before dying, he revealed that he left his **One Piece** treasure (“everything this world has to offer”) at the Grand Line. Many pirates started looking for this treasure, initiating the **Great Pirate Era**.

The highest authority in the World Government is the **Gorousei** (The five elderly stars), a counsel of 5 elders, in **Mariejoa**, in the Sacred Land. They control the **3 great powers**, which reign over the Grand Line, making it deadly for pirates:

- **Sichibukai**: The 7 Warlords of the sea. A group of government-authorized pirates, since they pay a cut of the loot they get. Some of them are: Hawk-eye, Jimbei, Crocodile, Don Quijote Doflamingo, Bartholomew Kuma.

## Luffy

**Monkey D. Luffy** was a boy that wanted to find the One Piece and become the King of Pirates. When he was a kid (7 yo) living at **Foosha village**, in **Dawn island**, he met pirate **Shanks**, leader of the **Red Hair pirates**. They were carrying a Devil’s fruit (gum fruit), but Luffy ate it by mistake and became a very strong rubber person. Both became friends and Shanks gave Luffy his straw hat before going back to the sea.

Luffy (7 yo) befriended a kid called **Ace** (10 yo), son of Gold Roger, and both became sworn brothers. When Ace was 17 yo he left the island to become a pirate, and 3 years later (20 years after the beginning of the Great Pirate Era) Luffy tried to do the same. He got a ship but it was destroyed by a whirlpool and he was rescued by some passenger ship.

## New friends

**Coby** was a boy that was captured by pirate Alvida one day while he was fishing, and was forced to serve at his ship for 2 years. Luffy defeated Alvida when she attacked the passenger ship where he was, and became friend of Coby.

**Roronoa Zoro** was a pirate hunter and great swordman (expert in the art of three swords) that was imprisioned at the **Navy base** city island (the Navy headquarters), for defying the Navy. The Navy was in charge of chasing pirates. Luffy was looking for a crew, so he rescued Zoro, and both defeated **Morgan**, the captain in charge of that base. Zoro joined Lufy, but Coby decided to join the Navy.

**Nami** was an 18 years old navigator who stole from pirates. Her goal was to get 100 million Berries for unknown reasons.

Luffy and Zoro left the island in a small boat and arrived to a town that was being pillaged by **Buggy the Clown**, a pirate who had eaten a Devil’s fruit (chop fruit), which allowed him to be cut into pieces and put together again. Both fought Buggy and his crew, and defeated them, and Luffy threw Buggy far away. Nami, who was trying to steal from Buggy, joined them. From Buggy they took a chart of the Grand Line (Buggy stole it from the Navy base island) and a treasure (~5 million berries) and they left the island with their small boat and Buggy’s boat. 

Then, they arrived to a village in an island where they met **Usopp**, the son of Yasopp (one of Shanks men). Soon, the **Black Cat pirates** arrived, leaded by Captain **Kuro**, a very fast man. His plan was to take the fortune from the richest person in the town: a young girl called **Kaya**. Luffy, Zoro, Nami, and Usopp joined forces to stop them before they could reach the town, and defeated them. As a thank you, Kaya gave them a ship called **Going Merry**. Usopp joined Luffy’s crew as their cannon sniper.

## The Straw Hat pirates

Nami guided them to the **Treasure island**, but they found no treasure, just a bunch of empty chests. They also met the only human living in the island: **Gaimon**, a former pirate.

In the sea, they met the **Pirate Bounty Hunter Duo** (**Johnny** & **Yosaku**), former colleages of Zoro, who joined the crew. Luffy wanted to recruit a cook, so the pirates took them to **Baratie**, a restaurant ship leaded by **Red Food Zeff**, a former pirate that in the past spent one year in the Grand Line.

**Commodore Don Krieg**, the East Blue ruler, leader of a fleet of 50 ships and 5000 men, tried to reach the Grand Line but he met **Hawk-eye** (Dracule Mihawk), a sichibukai and the world’s best swordman, who destroyed Krieg’s fleet alone. 

Zoro, who’s ambition was to be the strongest swordman, challenged Hawk-eye to a duel, but Zoro was easily defeated. Hawk-eye revealed that the Grand Line was crawling with powerful people, and that the East Blue was the weakest sea. Then, he abandoned the place. Meanwhile, Nami escaped alone with the Going Merry towards her home island, **Cocoyashi island**.

Krieg, having no more ships, tried to capture Baratie. It was defended by the cookers and Luffy. After a hard fight, he defeated Krieg and the pirates left the place. **Sanji**, a cook with a very strong kick, joined Luffy’s crew (he wanted to get to the All Blue). As thanks, they gave Luffy another ship, and they left in the same direction as Nami. Zeff gave him his logbook containing a record of his stance at the Grand Line.

Fishmen are evolved humans that can breathe underwater and that are usually 10 times stronger than normal humans. **Arlong** was a fishman pirate right on par with Jimbei (a fishman sichibukai) that used to rampage throughout the Grand Line, and that rules over 20 towns in the East Blue. Nami used to live in Cocoyashi village with her stepmother and stepsister (**Nojiko**). Eight years ago, Arlong captured the village and killed her stepmother for not being able to pay taxes to him. Nami agreed with Arlong on buying Cocoyashi from him for 100 million berries, but meanwhile she had to join Arlong pirates as their navigator.

Now Nami had gathered 93 million berries, which hid underground close to his house at Cocoyashi. However, Arlong was secretly working with some members of the Navy and told them that Nami had a hidden treasure, so they showed up at her house and took her treasure by force (Arlong had granted them a 30%). Then, Luffy and his crew decided to fight Arlong. It was a hard fight, but Luffy finally defeated him. Then, Zoro easily defeated the Navy members allied with Arlong. The stolen treasure was recovered and Nami gave it to her village. Johnny and Yosaku decided to stay at the village, but Nami joined Luffy’s crew again and they sailed towards the Grand Line as **the Straw Hat pirates**.

## Near the Grand Line

Luffy’s actions so far had been noticed by the Navy, and it put a bounty on him (30 millions). He was starting to be famous. 

Luffy decided to visit **Loguetown**, the closest city to the Grand Line, where Gold Roger was born and executed. **Captain Smoker**, a Navy captain with Devil’s fruit powers (plum/smoke fruit), was in charge of the city.

Buggy and Alvida, who joined forces and were looking for Luffy, found him at the city and tried to get their revenge. This time, Alvida had eaten a Devil’s fruit (smooth fruit), which made her skin highly slippery, making her difficult to hurt. Captain Smoker also tried to defeat Luffy. However, a man called **Dragon** saved him and let him escape from the island. Luffy and his crew sailed towards the Grand Line.

In the sea, they found a little girl (**Apis**, from **Warship island**) in a boat, who recently escaped from commodore **Nelson Royal**. She was his prisioner because she knew about dragonite, the elixir of immortality (made up from the bones of the old Millennial Dragons that used to live at **Lost island**). Luffy took her to Warship island where he discovered that a lost dragon was living there and that Apis was secretly taking care of him. Furthermore, Apis had the whisper fruit power, which made she capable of reading animal’s minds.

Some Navy troops disembarked looking for the dragon, but Luffy and his crew managed to take it to their ship and escaped towards Lost island. They found an abandoned island with ruins and paintings revealing that Warship island was Lost island. They headed back to Warship island, but Nelson’s fleet surrounded and attacked them. They also fought **Eric**, a mercenary with the Sickle fruit powers hired by Nelson to get the dragon. During the battle, the surroundings of the island emerged, and a flock of dragons arrived. This event happens every 1000 years, and dragons migrate to it when that happens. The dragons attacked the enemy fleet, and Luffy destroyed the flagship. Unfortunately, their dragon friend died during the Navy’s attack.

Apis decided to stay in Lost island, and the Straw Hat pirates continued their travel towards the Grand Line. They got to Reverse mountain and accessed through one its channels to the Grand Line.

At the entrance to the Grand Line they found a lighthouse, and became friends of the lighthouse keeper and his giant whale (biggest whale of the world). The islands at the Grand Line have magnetic minerals that prevent compasses from working. The keeper gave them a **log pose**, a special compass that points a way through some islands along the Grand Line. At the end of this path of islands lies **Raftel island**, only confirmed by Gold Roger, and where it’s thought his treasure lies, although nobody has reached it. To be able to follow the path of islands, the log pose has to stay some time in each island in order to get enough magnetism to point to the next island in the route.

## Into the Grand Line

Straw Hat pirates used the log pose to navigate through the Grand Line. The log pose took them to an island with huge cacti and the **Whiskey Peak town**. However, this place was one of the bases of **Baroque Works**, a criminal syndicate, which tried to capture them to get Luffy’s bounty. However, Zoro and Luffy easily stopped them. They defeated two enemies with Devil’s fruit powers: **Mr. 5** (Bomb fruit) and **Miss Valentine** (Kilo fruit). **Nefertari Vivi**, princess of **Arabasta Kingdom**, was infiltrated in Baroque Works ranks because they were spreading civil war in her kingdom. However, she was discovered, but Straw Hat pirates saved her. She wanted to go back home, so she and his big duck, **Karoo**, joined them. She told them that Baroque Works’ leader was **Crocodile** (**Mr. 0**), the 7th warlord of the sea.

Meanwhile, Coby and his friend **Helmeppo** (son of the former captain Morgan), who were working as chores boys for the Navy, were taken by **Vice-Admiral Garp** to the **Navy-Headquarters**, at the Grand Line, where they started training hard.

Straw Hat pirates arrived to **Little Garden**. They became friends of the only two people living in the island: **Broggy** and **Dorry**, two giants from the country of **Elbaf** that had been living in the island for 100 years. Soon, some members of Baroque Works arrived: **Mr. 3** (Wax fruit), **Miss Golden Week** (hipnotizing powers), Mr. 5, and Miss Valentine. They tried to kill Straw Hat pirates and capture the giants (there was an old bounty of 100 million berries on each one because they were leaders of the **Giant Warriors pirates**). Their next step was to attack Arabasta kingdom. However, they were defeated, and Sanji could steal from them an **Eternal log** (type of log pose that permanently points to a specific island and needs no time to recharge magnetism), which pointed to Arabasta kingdom.

Straw hat pirates continued their journey. Nami got sick and had a fever. They were attacked by **Wapol** (Munch fruit) and his pirates, but they defeated him easily. Then, they arrived to **Big Horn village**, in the snowy **Drum island**, where **Dalton** (Ox fruit), the captain of the security squad of the island, kindly offered his house to them. Dalton explained that the island was a country called **Drum kingdom**, governed by king Wapol, until a few months ago a small group of 5 powerful pirates leaded by **Blackbeard** arrived to the island and destroyed the country. Wapol escaped to the sea (where Straw Hat pirates met him). There was only one doctor in the island, **Dr. Kureha** (139 years old), which was living at the top of 5 kms height mountain, in Wapol’s castle, so Luffy and Sanji took Nami there. In their way, they had to fight some dangerous rabbits (**Lapins**) and escaped from an avalanche caused by them. Kureha cured Nami. She was ill because a bacteria (**Kestia**) infected her at Little Garden.

Kureha was living with **Chopper**, a reindeer with human abilities (speak and become human-like) since he ate the Human fruit. Soon, Wapol and his army arrived to the island. He was an awful king, so Dalton and Big Horn’s people tried to stop him without success, and Dalton almost die. Then, Wapol arrived to his former castle and Luffy and Chopper fought and defeated him. Chopper joined them, and the Straw Hat pirates continued their journey.

## Arabasta kingdom

Arabasta kingdom, at **Sandy island**, was in the middle of a civil war. The royal army was losing the fight against the **Resistance**. Crocodile was considered a hero in Arabasta, even by king **Nefertari Cobra** (Vivi’s father), because he helped to get rid of pirates. But he was evil inside, he was secretly driving the civil war with the purpose of taking down the kingdom. His organization, Baroque Works, had 2000 men.

Meanwhile, Ace (flame fruit), sworn brother of Luffy and now commander of the 2nd division of **Whitebeard’s pirates** (Whitebeard, or Edward Newgate, is considered the strongest man in the world), was in a search and kill mission of Blackbeard, a former subordinate of him who betrayed and killed some Whitebeard’s pirates.

The Straw Hat pirates finally arrived to Arabasta, to **Nanohana** city, where they got supplies. Vivi’s plan was to meet the rebel’s leader, **Kohza**, a childhood friend, and convince him to stop fighting the king and defeat Crocodile instead. The kingdom was suffering a severe drought for 3 years. Some Navy troops leaded by Captain Smoker, and major sergeant **Tashigi**, were at Nanohana looking for the Straw Hat pirates. They almost capture the pirates, but Ace saved them. Vivi sent Karoo with a letter for the king revealing him that Crocodile was the real enemy. Then they left Nanohana and navigated towards **Erumalu** city. On their way, Ace destroyed a Baroque Works fleet that tried to attack them. Erumalu turned out to be abandoned due to the drought. After finding out that Blackbeard was not in Arabasta, Ace left the group to continue his search in the west.

They continued through the desert towards **Yuba oasis**, where they thought the rebel leader was, but once there they discovered that the rebels had moved to **Karotea** city, so they decided to continue towards **Rainbase** city, where Crocodile was, to attack him. However, once in Rainbase, the pirates, Vivi and Smoker were captured by Crocodile (sand fruit), except for Sanji and Chopper.

Crocodile secretly expected the kingdom to collapse so he could take control of it. He had caused the drought with his sand powers. And with lies and malicious actions he had decreased the popularity of the king. Ultimately, he kidnapped the king, provided weapons to the rebels, and induced them into attacking **Alubarna**, the capital city and the king's home.

Sanji rescued the prisioners while Chopper was performing a distraction. The pirates and Vivi went to Alubarna to stop the clash between the rebels and the royal army, but Luffy was forced to stay and fight Crocodile.

Luffy was defeated, but survived. In Alubarna, the pirates and Vivi tried to stop the clash, but failed, and the battle started in the city. Crocodile arrive to Alubarna, planning to kill both armies by bombarding the city. Kohza discovered Crocodile's plan and that he was who provoked Arabasta ruin. He tried to stop the battle, but Baroque Works had members infiltrated in both sides that prevented it by causing confusion and fear.

Luffy arrived to Alubarna and fought Crocodile. Luffy used water to counter Crocodile's sand powers, but was defeated again. Crocodile was secretely looking for Pluto, the "most feared military power of the world", which was hidden somewhere in Arabasta. The Marines joined the fight to help the pirates save the city. Vivi and the pirates found Crocodile's bomb an **Pell**, an officer of the king with the ability to fly, sacrificed himself by taking it to the sky right before it exploded, saving the city. However, Pell survived.

Luffy fights Crocodile again, defeating him this time. The rain returns to Arabasta. The battle stopped and everybody realized that Crocodile was the real enemy. The marines captured Crocodile and their men (Baroque Works) and took them to their headquarters. Peace returns to the kingdom.

The pirates leave the island but the marines, leaded by captain **Hina** (cage fruit), try to capture them in the sea. They escape with the help of **Mr. 2**, or **Bon Clay** (copy fruit), an ex-Baroque Works member that became their friend.

**Nico Robin** (flower fruit), an ex-Baroque Works member that saved Luffy twice from dying when fighting Crocodile, joins the pirates. She's an archeologist looking for the **River Poneglyph**. 

## The Rainbow mist

Next events:

- The pirates arrived to an uninhabited island, gathered resources, and continued their journey.
- They arrived to an island where they got supplies at a town and witnessed local fireworks.
- They arrived to a small island and become friends with the only person living there, an old castaway who lives with the island's goats. The marines attack the pirates in the island, but they escape to the sea.
- The marines leaded by Major **Pasqua** found them, but the pirates manage to escape.

They pirates arrive to an island, where they visited the town of **Ruluka**. They met scientist **Henzo**, who was studying the "Rainbow mist", which coincidentally appeared near the island shortly after. The pirates and Henzo went to explore it. The mist took them to another place (called Ape's Concert), outside regular time and space. Time there flows much slower, and escaping is difficult. There were many ships with treasures (ship graveyard) and a few kids trapped there. The kids were old friends of Henzo that got inside the mist 50 years ago and didn't grow up. Ruluka's major, **Whetton**, entered the mist with his men to get its treasures. Whetton was a pirate that attacked Ruluka 50 years ago but, after losing his ship in the rainbow mist, he became Ruluka's major. Finally, everybody escaped from the mist and Whetton was captured by some marines for trying to keep the treasures instead of giving them to the World Government. These marines turned out to be the Henzo's friends, who returned from the mist to 50 years ago and became marines. 

## The sky island

In the sea, the log pose points to the sky. Robin tells that she read in a book that there's an island in the sky, though it's not visible. Shortly after, a big ship falls from the sky. It's the Sent Blis, a 200 yo ship, broken and with corpses. From it, the pirates get a map of **Skypiea**, an island of the sky. They also meet **Masira** and his pirates, who came there to get the old ship. However, the day became dark all of a sudden and huge unknown monsters got close, so both pirate groups escaped.

Using an eternal log that Robin stole from Masira, the pirates get to **Mocktown**, in **Jaya** island, an island of pirates, looking for a way to get to the island in the sky. However, nobody there believed such island exists. Pirate **Bellamy** (spring fruit) explains that there're many currents, and one of them is the Knock Up stream (caused by underground geotermal events) which takes ships to the sky to make them fall later. This made some people think that there was an island in the sky. However, the ex-pirate **Montblanc Cricket**, friend of Masira and his brother **Soho**, tell them about the Cumuloregalis, a especial type of cloud that doesn't let light go through and keeps the same shape for millenia. So, if the island exists, that's the only place where it could be.

With the help of Cricket, Masira, Soho, and their pirates, the straw hat pirates got into the Knock Up stream and reached a sea in the sky: the White Sea, which is 7.000 meters above sea level. They met **Gan Fall**, the Sky's knight, who rides a bird called **Pierre** that can transform into a Pegaso (this bird ate the horse fruit). He told them that 10.000 meters above sea level was the White-White Sea. Later, they found a gate that took them up there and arrived to Skypiea island.

They met a girl called **Conis** and his father. They explain that there're two types of cloud there: marine clouds (similar to liquid) and island clouds (more solid). In the sky, they make great use of dials (seashells with different properties like sound recording, propulsion, heat...). While using the weaver (an auto-propulsed single-person vehicle) of Conis' father, Nami arrived to Upper Yard, a sacred and forbidden place where the God **Enel** lives. God Enel governs Skypiea.
