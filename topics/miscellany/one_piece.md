## Introduction

**Geography**:

In the world there are many island and only one continent, the **Red Line**, a high mountain range with huge cliffs that circles the planet from north to south. The **Grand Line** is an oceanic current that goes along the ecuator. The Red Line and the Grand Line surround the **4 seas**: North Blue, South Blue, West Blue, East blue (where this story begins). The **All blue** is a mythical sea somewhere. The Grand Line is surrounded by two parallel belts where there is no wind and no current (**Calm Belt**), home of huge sea monsters (**Sea Kings**). In the Red Line there's the **Reverse mountain**, which has 5 channels that connect the Grand Line and the 4 seas at the top, and serve as a point of access to any of these regions.

<br>![map_1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AnselmoGPP/know_base/master/topics/miscellany/resources/one_piece_2.png)

**History**:

**Gold D. Roger**, the King of Pirates, got arrested and executed by the **World Government**. Before dying, he revealed that he left his **One Piece** treasure (“_everything this world has to offer_”) at the Grand Line. Many pirates started looking for this treasure, initiating the **Great Pirate Era**.

**Government**:

The World Government is an alliance of 170 countries. It was founded 800 years ago by 20 kings. Its highest authority is the **Gorousei** (The five elderly stars), a counsel of 5 elders, in the Sacred Land of **Mary Geoise**, in the Red Line. They control the **3 great powers**, which reign over the Grand Line, making it deadly for pirates. These powers are:

- The **Sichibukai**, the 7 Warlords of the sea. This is a group of government-authorized pirates, since they pay a cut of the loot they get. They are: Hawk-eye, Jimbei, Crocodile, Don Quixote Doflamingo, Bartholomew Kuma, Gecko Moria, Boa Hancock.
- The **Marine**. An army leaded by various viceadmirals, 3 admirals (Aokiji, Kizaru, Sakazuki), and the General Admiral (Sengoku).
- X

The World Government controls one half of the Grand Line, but the other (called New World) belongs to strong pirates. Though the Government tried to conquer it, his forces were defeated.

**Devil's fruit**:

The **Devil's fruit** (Akuma no mi) is a rare type of fruit that gives powers to those who eat it, but takes away their ability to swim. However, eating two fruits cause death. There're many types of them, but only one of each type. The **Kairouseki** (seastone) is a crystallized form of the sea that temporarilly suppresses the powers of people that have eaten a devil's fruit.

## New friends

(chapters 1-17)

**Monkey D. Luffy** was a boy that wanted to find the One Piece and become the King of Pirates. When he was a 7 years old kid living at **Foosha village**, in **Dawn island**, at East Blue, he met pirate **Shanks**, leader of the **Red Hair pirates**. They were carrying a Devil’s fruit (gum fruit), but Luffy ate it by mistake and became a very strong rubber person. When a bandit threw Luffy into the sea, Shanks saved him, but lost an arm to a Sea King. Both became friends and Shanks gave Luffy his straw hat before going back to the sea.

Luffy (7 years old) befriended a kid called **Ace** (10 years old), and both became sworn brothers. When Ace was 17 years old he left the island to become a pirate, and 3 years later (20 years after the beginning of the Great Pirate Era) Luffy tried to do the same. He got a ship but it was destroyed by a whirlpool and he was rescued by some passenger ship.

**Coby** was a boy that was captured by pirate Alvida one day while he was fishing, and was forced to serve at his ship for 2 years. Luffy defeated Alvida when she attacked the passenger ship where he was, and became friend of Coby.

**Roronoa Zoro** was a pirate hunter and great swordman (expert in the art of three swords) that was imprisioned at the **Navy base** city island (the Navy headquarters), for defying the Navy. The Navy was in charge of chasing pirates. Luffy was looking for a crew, so he rescued Zoro, and both defeated **Morgan**, the captain in charge of that base. Zoro joined Lufy, but Coby decided to join the Navy.

**Nami** was an 18 years old navigator who stole from pirates. Her goal was to get 100 million Berries for unknown reasons.

Luffy and Zoro left the island in a small boat and arrived to a town that was being pillaged by **Buggy the Clown**, a pirate who had eaten a Devil’s fruit (chop fruit), which allowed him to be cut into pieces and put together again. Both fought Buggy and his crew, and defeated them, and Luffy threw Buggy far away. Nami, who was trying to steal from Buggy, joined them. From Buggy they took a chart of the Grand Line (Buggy stole it from the Navy base island) and a treasure (~5 million berries) and they left the island with their small boat and Buggy’s boat. 

Then, they arrived to a village in an island where they met **Usopp**, the son of Yasopp (one of Shanks men). Soon, the **Black Cat pirates** arrived, leaded by Captain **Kuro**, a very fast man. His plan was to take the fortune from the richest person in the town: a young girl called **Kaya**. Luffy, Zoro, Nami, and Usopp joined forces to stop them before they could reach the town, and defeated them. As a thank you, Kaya gave them a caravel called **Going Merry**. Usopp joined Luffy’s crew as their cannon sniper.

## The Straw Hat pirates

(chapters 18-44)

Nami guided them to the **Treasure island**, but they found no treasure, just a bunch of empty chests. They also met the only human living in the island: **Gaimon**, a former pirate.

In the sea, they met the **Pirate Bounty Hunter Duo** (**Johnny** & **Yosaku**), former colleages of Zoro, who joined the crew. Luffy wanted to recruit a cook, so the pirates took them to **Baratie**, a restaurant ship leaded by **Red Food Zeff**, a former pirate that in the past spent one year in the Grand Line.

**Commodore Don Krieg**, the East Blue ruler, leader of a fleet of 50 ships and 5000 men, tried to reach the Grand Line but he met **Hawk-eye** (Dracule Mihawk), a sichibukai and the world’s best swordman, who destroyed Krieg’s fleet alone. 

Zoro, who’s ambition was to be the strongest swordman, challenged Hawk-eye to a duel, but Zoro was easily defeated. Hawk-eye revealed that the Grand Line was crawling with powerful people, and that the East Blue was the weakest sea. Then, he abandoned the place. Meanwhile, Nami escaped alone with the Going Merry towards her home island, **Cocoyashi island**.

Krieg, having no more ships, tried to capture Baratie. It was defended by the cookers and Luffy. After a hard fight, he defeated Krieg and the pirates left the place. **Sanji**, a cook with a very strong kick, joined Luffy’s crew (he wanted to get to the All Blue). As thanks, they gave Luffy another ship, and they left in the same direction as Nami. Zeff gave him his logbook containing a record of his stance at the Grand Line.

Fishmen are evolved humans that can breathe underwater and that are usually 10 times stronger than normal humans. **Arlong** was a fishman pirate right on par with Jimbei (a fishman sichibukai) that used to rampage throughout the Grand Line, and that rules over 20 towns in the East Blue. Nami used to live in Cocoyashi village with her stepmother and stepsister (**Nojiko**). Eight years ago, Arlong captured the village and killed her stepmother for not being able to pay taxes to him. Nami agreed with Arlong on buying Cocoyashi from him for 100 million berries, but meanwhile she had to join Arlong pirates as their navigator.

Now Nami had gathered 93 million berries, which hid underground close to his house at Cocoyashi. However, Arlong was secretly working with some members of the Navy and told them that Nami had a hidden treasure, so they showed up at her house and took her treasure by force (Arlong had granted them a 30%). Then, Luffy and his crew decided to fight Arlong. It was a hard fight, but Luffy finally defeated him. Then, Zoro easily defeated the Navy members allied with Arlong. The stolen treasure was recovered and Nami gave it to her village. Johnny and Yosaku decided to stay at the village, but Nami joined Luffy’s crew again and they sailed towards the Grand Line as **the Straw Hat pirates**.

Luffy’s actions so far had been noticed by the Navy, and it put a bounty on him (30 million Berries). He was starting to be famous.

## Near the Grand Line

(chapters 45-63)

Luffy decided to visit **Loguetown**, the closest city to the Grand Line, where Gold Roger was born and executed. **Captain Smoker**, a Navy captain with Devil’s fruit powers (plum/smoke fruit), was in charge of the city.

Buggy and Alvida, who joined forces and were looking for Luffy, found him at the city and tried to get their revenge. This time, Alvida had eaten a Devil’s fruit (smooth fruit), which made her skin highly slippery, making her difficult to hurt. Captain Smoker also tried to defeat Luffy. However, a man called **Dragon** saved him and let him escape from the island. Luffy and his crew sailed towards the Grand Line.

In the sea, they found a little girl (**Apis**, from **Warship island**) in a boat, who recently escaped from commodore **Nelson Royal**. She was his prisioner because she knew about dragonite, the elixir of immortality (made up from the bones of the old Millennial Dragons that used to live at **Lost island**). Luffy took her to Warship island where he discovered that a lost dragon was living there and that Apis was secretly taking care of him. Furthermore, Apis had the whisper fruit power, which made she capable of reading animal’s minds.

Some Navy troops disembarked looking for the dragon, but Luffy and his crew managed to take it to their ship and escaped towards Lost island. They found an abandoned island with ruins and paintings revealing that Warship island was Lost island. They headed back to Warship island, but Nelson’s fleet surrounded and attacked them. They also fought **Eric**, a mercenary with the Sickle fruit powers hired by Nelson to get the dragon. During the battle, the surroundings of the island emerged, and a flock of dragons arrived. This event happens every 1000 years, and dragons migrate to it when that happens. The dragons attacked the enemy fleet, and Luffy destroyed the flagship. Unfortunately, their dragon friend died during the Navy’s attack.

Apis decided to stay in Lost island, and the Straw Hat pirates continued their travel towards the Grand Line. They got to Reverse mountain and accessed through one its channels to the Grand Line.

At the entrance to the Grand Line (**Twin Capes**) they found a lighthouse, and became friends of **Crocus**, the lighthouse keeper, and **Laboon**, his giant whale (biggest whale of the world). The islands at the Grand Line have magnetic minerals that prevent compasses from working. The keeper gave them a **log pose**, a special compass that points a way through some islands along the Grand Line (compasses doesn't work in the Grand Line due to the magnetism between islands). At the end of this path of islands lies **Raftel island**, only confirmed by Gold Roger, and where it’s thought his treasure lies, although nobody has reached it. To be able to follow the path of islands, the log pose has to stay some time in each island in order to get enough magnetism to point to the next island in the route.

## Into the Grand Line

(chapters 64-91)

Straw Hat pirates used the log pose to navigate through the Grand Line. The log pose took them to an island with huge cacti and the **Whiskey Peak town**. However, this place was one of the bases of **Baroque Works**, a criminal syndicate, which tried to capture them to get Luffy’s bounty. However, Zoro and Luffy easily stopped them. They defeated two enemies with Devil’s fruit powers: **Mr. 5** (Bomb fruit) and **Miss Valentine** (Kilo fruit). **Nefertari Vivi**, princess of **Arabasta Kingdom**, was infiltrated in Baroque Works ranks because they were spreading civil war in her kingdom. However, she was discovered, but Straw Hat pirates saved her. She wanted to go back home, so she and his big duck, **Karoo**, joined them. She told them that Baroque Works’ leader was **Crocodile** (**Mr. 0**), the 7th warlord of the sea.

Meanwhile, Coby and his friend **Helmeppo** (son of the former captain Morgan), who were working as chores boys for the Navy, were taken by **Viceadmiral Garp** to the **Navy-Headquarters**, at the Grand Line, where they started training hard.

Straw Hat pirates arrived to **Little Garden**. They became friends of the only two people living in the island: **Broggy** and **Dorry**, two giants from the country of **Elbaf** that had been living in the island for 100 years. Soon, some members of Baroque Works arrived: **Mr. 3** (Wax fruit), **Miss Golden Week** (hipnotizing powers), Mr. 5, and Miss Valentine. They tried to kill Straw Hat pirates and capture the giants (there was an old bounty of 100 million berries on each one because they were leaders of the **Giant Warriors pirates**). Their next step was to attack Arabasta kingdom. However, they were defeated, and Sanji could steal from them an **Eternal log** (type of log pose that permanently points to a specific island and needs no time to recharge magnetism), which pointed to Arabasta kingdom.

Straw hat pirates continued their journey. Nami got sick and had a fever. They were attacked by **Wapol** (Munch fruit) and his pirates, but they defeated him easily. Then, they arrived to **Big Horn village**, in the snowy **Drum island**, where **Dalton** (Ox fruit), the captain of the security squad of the island, kindly offered his house to them. Dalton explained that the island was a country called **Drum kingdom**, governed by king Wapol, until a few months ago a small group of 5 powerful pirates leaded by **Blackbeard** (Marshall D. Teach) arrived to the island and destroyed the country. Wapol escaped to the sea (where Straw Hat pirates met him). There was only one doctor in the island, **Dr. Kureha** (139 years old), which was living at the top of 5 kms height mountain, in Wapol’s castle, so Luffy and Sanji took Nami there. In their way, they had to fight some dangerous rabbits (**Lapins**) and escaped from an avalanche caused by them. Kureha cured Nami. She was ill because a bacteria (**Kestia**) infected her at Little Garden.

Kureha was living with **Chopper**, a reindeer with human abilities (speak and become human-like) since he ate the Human fruit. Soon, Wapol and his army arrived to the island. He was an awful king, so Dalton and Big Horn’s people tried to stop him without success, and Dalton almost die. Then, Wapol arrived to his former castle and Luffy and Chopper fought and defeated him. Chopper joined them, and the Straw Hat pirates continued their journey.

## Arabasta kingdom

(chapters 92-130)

Arabasta kingdom, at **Sandy island**, was in the middle of a civil war. The royal army was losing the fight against the **Resistance**. Crocodile was considered a hero in Arabasta, even by king **Nefertari Cobra** (Vivi’s father), because he helped to get rid of pirates. But he was evil inside, he was secretly driving the civil war with the purpose of taking down the kingdom. His organization, Baroque Works, had 2000 men.

Meanwhile, Ace (flame fruit), sworn brother of Luffy and now 2nd division commander of **Whitebeard’s pirates** (Whitebeard, or **Edward Newgate**, is considered the strongest man in the world), was in a search and kill mission of Blackbeard, a former subordinate of him who betrayed and killed some Whitebeard’s pirates.

The Straw Hat pirates finally arrived to Arabasta, to **Nanohana** city, where they got supplies. Vivi’s plan was to meet the rebel’s leader, **Kohza**, a childhood friend, and convince him to stop fighting the king and defeat Crocodile instead. The kingdom was suffering a severe drought for 3 years. Some Navy troops leaded by Captain Smoker, and major sergeant **Tashigi**, were at Nanohana looking for the Straw Hat pirates. They almost capture the pirates, but Ace saved them. Vivi sent Karoo with a letter for the king revealing him that Crocodile was the real enemy. Then they left Nanohana and navigated towards **Erumalu** city. On their way, Ace destroyed a Baroque Works fleet that tried to attack them. Erumalu turned out to be abandoned due to the drought. After finding out that Blackbeard was not in Arabasta, Ace left the group to continue his search in the west.

They continued through the desert towards **Yuba oasis**, where they thought the rebel leader was, but once there they discovered that the rebels had moved to **Karotea** city, so they decided to continue towards **Rainbase** city, where Crocodile was, to attack him. However, once in Rainbase, the pirates, Vivi and Smoker were captured by Crocodile (sand fruit), except for Sanji and Chopper.

Crocodile secretly expected the kingdom to collapse so he could take control of it. He had caused the drought with his sand powers. And with lies and malicious actions he had decreased the popularity of the king. Ultimately, he kidnapped the king, provided weapons to the rebels, and induced them into attacking **Alubarna**, the capital city and the king's home.

Sanji rescued the prisioners while Chopper was performing a distraction. The pirates and Vivi went to Alubarna to stop the clash between the rebels and the royal army, but Luffy was forced to stay and fight Crocodile.

Luffy was defeated, but survived. In Alubarna, the pirates and Vivi tried to stop the clash, but failed, and the battle started in the city. Crocodile arrive to Alubarna, planning to kill both armies by bombarding the city. Kohza discovered Crocodile's plan and that he was who provoked Arabasta ruin. He tried to stop the battle, but Baroque Works had members infiltrated in both sides that prevented it by causing confusion and fear.

Luffy arrived to Alubarna and fought Crocodile. Luffy used water to counter Crocodile's sand powers, but was defeated again. Crocodile was secretely looking for Pluton, the "most feared military power of the world", which was hidden somewhere in Arabasta. The Marines joined the fight to help the pirates save the city. Zoro defeated **Mr. 1** (Daz Bonez) (Blade fruit) after a complicated fight. Vivi and the pirates found Crocodile's bomb, and **Pell**, an officer of the king with the ability to fly, sacrificed himself by taking it to the sky right before it exploded, saving the city. Fortunately, Pell survived.

Luffy fights Crocodile again, defeating him this time. The rain returns to Arabasta. The battle stopped and everybody realized that Crocodile was the real enemy. The marines captured Crocodile and their men (Baroque Works) and took them to their headquarters. Peace returned to the kingdom.

A bounty is put on Zoro (60 million), and Luffy's bounty increases (100 million). The pirates leave the island but the marines, leaded by captain **Hina** (cage fruit), try to capture them in the sea. They escape with the help of **Mr. 2**, or **Bon Clay** (copy fruit), an ex-Baroque Works member that became their friend.

**Nico Robin** (flower fruit), an ex-Baroque Works member that saved Luffy twice from dying when fighting Crocodile, joins the pirates. She's an archeologist that wants to uncover the true history of the world, specifically the Void Century, by deciphering the **River Poneglyph**. In Arabasta she found a Poneglyph (unbreakable stone with an old language text) detailing where Pluton was, but she didn't tell Crocodile about this.

## The sky island

(chapters 144-195)

In the sea, the log pose points to the sky. Robin tells that she read in a book that there's an island in the sky, though it's not visible. Shortly after, a big ship falls from the sky. It's the Sent Blis (or Saint Bliss), a 200 years old ship, broken and with corpses. From it, the pirates get a map of **Skypiea**, a land of the sky. They also meet **Masira** and his pirates, who came there to get the old ship. However, the day became dark all of a sudden and they saw the silhouette of huge unknown monsters, so both pirate groups escaped.

Using an eternal log that Robin stole from Masira, the pirates get to **Mocktown**, in **Jaya** island, an island of pirates, looking for a way to get to the island in the sky. However, nobody there believed such island exists. Pirate **Bellamy** (spring fruit) explains that there're many currents, and one of them is the **Knock Up stream** (huge water jet caused by underground geotermal activity) which takes ships to the sky to make them fall later. This made some people think that there was an island in the sky. However, the ex-pirate **Montblanc Cricket**, friend of Masira and his brother **Soho**, tells them about the Cumuloregalis, a especial type of cloud that doesn't let light go through and keeps the same shape for millenia. So, if the island exists, that's the only place where it could be.

Cricket is descendant of the explorer **Montblanc Norland**, who 400 year ago said that he had discovered a city of gold in Jaya island. The king travelled with Norland to see that city, but it had dissappeared. Norland was regarded as a liar and executed by the king. Cricket use to dive near the island trying to find the city underwater.

With the help of Cricket, Masira, Soho, and their pirates, the straw hat pirates got into the Knock Up stream and reached a sea in the sky: the White Sea, which is 7.000 meters above sea level. They met **Gan Fall**, the Sky's knight, who rides a bird that can transform into a Pegaso (this bird ate the horse fruit). He told them that 10.000 meters above sea level was the White-White Sea. Later, they found a gate that took them up there and arrived to Skypiea, which is composed of a few islands.

In **Angel island** they met a girl called **Conis** and his father, **Pagaya**. They explained many things about Skypiea. There're two types of cloud there: marine clouds (similar to liquid) and island clouds (more solid). The sky's people make great use of **dials** (seashells from the sky with different properties like sound recording, propulsion, heat...). They let Nami drive a **waver** (single-person vehicle powered by a dial).

**God Enel** (lightning fruit) governs Skypiea ("God" is the title of the governor of Skypiea). In Skypiea there're two islands: Angel island, made of clouds and inhabited by the sky people, and **Upper Yard**, a sacred and forbidden island made of actual ground and inhabited by Enel, his four **Priests** (**Satori**, **Shura**, **Gedatsu**, **Ohm**), and his 50 god's warriors. Using the mantra ability (haki), Enel and the priests can predict the moves of their opponents and feel their presence.

Since the pirates were illegaly in Skypiea, the **White Berets**, a police body controlled by the Priests, tried to capture them, but they were defeated. However, the Going Merry with Nami, Robin, Zoro, and Chopper was captured and taken to the sacrificial altar in the **Lost Forest** of Upper Yard for the priests to sacrifice them. Luffy, Usopp, and Sanji went to Upper Yard to rescue them, and defeated Satori. Exploring the forest, Nami discovered that Upper Yard was a piece of the Jaya island that was taken up by the Knock Up stream about 400 year ago. Gan Fall joined the pirates to help them.

The **Shandians** (people of Shandia) are people that live in a village hidden in the clouds and are at war with Angel island and Upper Yard. They took advantage of the moment and attacked Upper Yard leaded by **Wiper**, but they were defeated by Ohm.

Meanwhile, all the Straw Hat pirates managed to get together and decided look in the island for the old abandoned city of **Shandora**, which was made of gold according to a legend. Gan Fall explained that 400 years ago, the sky's people were living normally until the Upper Yard appeared. The sky's people took control of the island, expelling the original population, the Shandians. Gan Fall was god of Skypiea until 6 year ago, until Enel and his priests arrived from another island of the sky and took control of Skypiea. That's wy Shandians are at war with the sky's people and god.

The Shandians attacked again, this time defeating Shura. Enel sent his 50 god's warriors to stop them, and a battle began where both sides suffered many casualties. Enel joined the fight and burned down Usopp and Sanji. Robin defeated the god's warriors chief. Luffy was swallowed by a giant snake. Chopper defeated Gedatsu, but he was later defeated by Ohm, who in turn was defeated by Zoro. After all god's warriors and priests were defeated, the last ones standing were Zoro, Robin, Nami, Wiper, and Gan Fall. They fought Enel, but they were defeated since Enel was made of electricity.

Enel's secret plan was to destroy Skypiea, since it considers its existence unnatural, and return to his place of birth, the **Fairy Vearth**, another sky island (although some people say he was born in Bilka, an sky island that he destroyed), using a flying ship that he forced his prisioners to build. This ship flies using Enel's electricity and carries the gold of Shandora.

Luffy escaped from the snake's stomach and fought Enel on his flying ship. Since Luffy is made of rubber, electricity doesn't affects him, so he is immune to Enel's electric attacks. However, Luffy was defeated and thrown overboard. Enel began destroying Skypiea throwing lightning bolts from the sky. Angel island was wiped out. Luffy managed to get to the ship again, this time defeating Enel.

Luffy rang the golden bell of Shandora, which was heard far away by the people of Jaya, including Cricket, realizing that Norland was not a liar. Enel escaped from Skypiea towards Fairy Vearth (the Moon). The sky people and the Shandians became friends and lived peacefully in Upper Yard, since Angel island dissappeared.

Robin found a Poneglyph under the golden bell detailing the location of an ancient weapon called Poseidon. Golden Roger was there and left a note, making Robin realize that the River Poneglyph is a collective message formed by assembling different poneglyphs scattered across the world. All them together form a path to Raftel. Gan Fall confirmed that Golden Roger was there 20 years ago.

The pirates took as much gold as they could. Usopp also took some dials (impact, breath, flame...), and Nami took a waver. Then, they went to the Cloud End and left Skypiea using a Balloon Octopus.

## Navarone

(chapters 196-206)

The pirates landed in **Navarone** (in an inner lake), a Navy's marine fortress run by **Commander Jonathan** and containing more than 1000 marines. ## Filler arcs

There're a number of additional stories inserted here and there in the main anime series:

- **Grand Jipangu**: Alternative story set in a feudal japan-inspired country.
- **Chopper man**: Alternative story about the antagonism between a hero (Chopper man) and a villain (Dr. Usodabada).
- **Amigo pirates**: Prelude to "One Piece film: Strong World".
- **One Piece & Toriko**: Special story for celebrating the starting of the anime Toriko.

## Grand Jipangu

Story set in an alternate reality, in a feudal japan-inspired town called **Grand Jipangu**. Main characters:

- **Luffy** (also, **Straw Hat**): Okkapiki (secret police from Edo period, detective) in the town. People often call him Oyabun (chief).
- **Sanji** and **Onami** (Nami) run the restaurant **Kazaguruma**.
- **Usopp**: Subordinate of Luffy
- Sensei (doctor) **Blue Nose** (Chopper)
- **Bou** (wandering monk, priest) (Zoro)
- **Princess** (Vivi): Shogun's daughter
- **Tono** (lord) (Nefertari Cobra): Shogun

(chapter 291)

The Clown family, leaded by **Buggy**, was a criminal group trying to take control of the town. The **bugyo** (magistrate, samurai official) (Mr. 3) of Kitamachi was bribed by Buggy to organize a conspiracy to get rid of Straw Hat. Buggy wanted to collect a debt from somebody called **Totsan** (meaning, father), so he captured **Orika** (friend of Luffy) to sell her as slave, thinking that she was Totsan's daughter. Straw Hat attacked him to prevent that, but the bugyo was present and considered that an unjustified attack, so he relieved him from his position (according to Buggy's and the bugyo's plan). However, Vivi, the Shogun's daughter, was present, and claimed that the bugyo was under investigation for accepting a bribe and conspiracy. The bugyo surrendered, Straw Hat expelled Buggy from the town. Later, Straw Hat, Orika, Totsan, and some friends celebrated Christmas (a foreign celebration) at the restaurant of Sanji and Onami.

(chapter 292)

In the new year, the race of Mochimaki (rice cake throwing) is celebrated at the town's castle. One mochi has inside a treasure called Kongou Seki valued at 10.000 ryou berries. All the mochis are thrown from the castle, and everybody below try to find the treasure mochi. Buggy wanted to get the treasure-mochi at all costs.

Onami, Sanji, and Sensei Blue Nose (Doctor Chopper) were carrying the mochis to the castle (Castle of Arabasta), but they got ambushed by the Clown family. They managed to run through the ambush, but shortly after they got ambushed again. This time, they were protected by Luffy, and the mochis were carried to the castle. The Clown family decided to attack the castle. They captured Vivi, trying to force his father **Tono** (lord) to hand over the treasure-mochi. At this point, Buggy changed his plan: now he wanted to kill Tono and rule the entire country. However, he was defeated, and the Mochimaki took place.

(chapter 303)

Once a year, in Spring, takes place the Hana-Mi festival (picnic under the cherry trees), where people of the town gather under a thousand-year-old cherry tree (the Sennen-Zakura). Nami's and Sanji's restaurant sold many bentou (food in a box). However, the Zakura disappeared misterously during the night. Robin discovered that the tree was stolen by pirates and hidden on a ship behind an island. The Clown family had stolen the tree for **Silver Fox** (Foxy), who wanted to sell it elsewhere. Luffy, Sanji, and Usopp attacked the pirates to recover the tree. Zoro, who was sleeping on the tree, joined the fight. The thieves were defeated, and Luffy expelled Foxy and Buggy.

(chapter 406-407)

Once a century, the race of mikoshis (portable Shinto shrines) is celebrated. The reward is one million ryou berries and the right to ask for one wish to the sacred treasure of the Great Temple. Luffy and his friends (Onami, Usopp, Chopper) wanted to participate, so they asked a carpenter (**Franky**) to build their shrine. Different companies joined the race, such as Kazaguruma (Luffy and his friends), Flying Riders, Rolling Express, and Thriller. However, Thriller company (Hogback, Absalom, Perona, Tararan, and zombies) destroyed the shrines of each participating company one by one, leaving Thriller as the only participant.

A new waitress was hired at Kazaguruma restaurant: **Omoi**.

While fishing in the river, Usopp caught a living eskeleton (**Brook**). He was a member of Thriller company that decided to leave the company after witnessing its dirty plays. However, he was sent to the bottom of the river for his betrayal. He became friend of Luffy and his friends. He was later captured by Thriller, but Luffy saved him with the help of Zoro and Robin (a girl that walks around).

That night, Luffy and his friends, with some help from the Flying Riders and the Rolling Express, rebuilt their shrine. The next day, the race took place with only 2 participants: Thriller and Kazaguruma. During the race, Thriller tried to eliminate Kazaguruma playing dirty, but they avoided their traps, and Luffy threw them far away from the town. Kazaguruma won the race.

(chapter 336)

## Amigo pirates

(chapters 426-429)

The SHPs arrived to the **Kansorn** island (or Little East Blue), inhabited by people from the East Blue. There was a giant beetle (**Boss**), friend of the inhabitants, especially **Yoko**, a little girl.

**Shiki** leads a group of 14 pirates. The **Amigo pirates**, leaded by captain Aniki (**Largo**) (Net fruit), and vice-captain Colt (**Corto**) want to join Shiki. They are allowed to join on the condition that they capture a giant beetle at Kansorn island that belonged to Shiki. ## One Piece & Toriko

(chapter 492)

The SHPs arrived to the **Gourmet island**, and island full of many types of ready-to-eat food. There they met **Toriko** (a hunter of rare ingredients and flavors for restaurants), **Komatsu** (a beginner cook), and a **Terry Cloth** (a battle wolf). Toriko went to the island looking for the **Gourmet-bird**. The SHPs joined Toriko. They were attacked by different beasts (Roast-porks, Unicorn-bears, and the Gourmet-bird). Finally, they defeated the Gourmet bird.

At night, a group of Cocoa-Koalas kidnapped Nami and Komatsu. They decided to save her by getting to their refuge: a big **Amata fruit** tree. In their way, they were attacked by a pack of Marshmallow-lions, but were saved by Toriko's friends **Sunny**, **Coco**, and **Rin**. Toriko, Terry, and the SWPs reached the refuge and fought the koalas, which were leaded by an adult koala: **Dotsukuzokoala** (a very strong koala).
