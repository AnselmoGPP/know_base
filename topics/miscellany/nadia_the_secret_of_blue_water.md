## Grandis Gang

**Jean** is 14 year old inventor from France. His father is a sailor that disappeared in the sea one year ago. Now he's living with his uncle, which is also an inventor. In Paris, at the 1889 Exposition Universelle, he meets **Nadia**, a 14 year old circus performer from Africa, and her inseparable lion cub, **King**.

The **Grandis Gang** is a group of jewel thieves whose members are: **Grandis Granva** (gang's leader, 28 years old), **Hanson** (genius, 27 years), and **Sanson** (strong man, 27 years old). Hanson built a big advanced all-terrain vehicle called **Gratan**. They try to steal a blue jewel that Nadia hangs around her neck (Blue Water). Jean and Nadia escape from them, but the Gang pursue them with Gratan. The kids get to Jean's workshop and escape flying in an ultralight aircraft built by Jean and his uncle. However, after a while, the aircraft stopped working properly and fell into the sea.

The kids and their aircraft was rescued by a nearby warship, the USS Abraham Lincoln, leaded by captain **Melville**. The warship was in a hunt for giant sea monsters that were atacking ships all over the world. In the ship, the kids met the scientist **Ayerton Grenavan**. Trying to get the jewel, the Gang attacked the warship, but they were easily defeated and captured. The warship was soon attacked by a sea monster and left immobilized. During the attack, the kids and the aircraft fell to the sea. The Gang and Gratan also fell to the sea and escaped.

The kids and their aircraft was rescued by the **Nautilus**, a submarine leaded by captain **Nemo**. Nemo was pursuing the sea monster, which was actually another submarine, but it escaped. The Nautilus crew made some improvements to Jean's aircraft and let the kids fly away.

Nadia didn't know where or when she was born, who her parents were, or where she got the Blue Water. However, she suspected she was from Africa due to her dark skin.

## Gargoyle

The kids approached to an island from where they could get back to France, but the island was overtaken by the **Neo-Atlantean** forces, a misterious army leaded by Lord **Gargoyle** (46 years old). They took down the aircraft with a rocket, and the kids crashed on it. They discovered the dead bodies of a man, a woman, and a dog, and rescued their surviving daughter, a 4 years old girl called **Marie**. The soldiers tried to kill them, but they escaped and hid in a cave. However, Nadia, Marie, and King were soon captured and brought to a Gargoyle's castle. Gargoyle was after the Blue Water, but Nadia gave it to Jean before being captured.

The soldiers had enslaved the island's population, and also the Grandis Gang, who had recently arrived to the island. They had established their base inside a volcanic crater. The army had advanced technology, and were the owners of the submarine that Nemo was pursuing. Futhermore, for 12 years they had been building a weapon of mass destruction (Tower of Babel) which could launch a laser beam capable of destroying anything they want on the Earth by using an ancient Atlantean artificial satellite system. They wanted to rebuild **Atlantis** and rule the world. Atlantis was a prosperous country 12.000 years ago.

They tested the weapon by using it to destroy a nearby island. The energy output was detected by the Nautilus, which calculated the origin of the beam and headed to the island.

The Grandis Gang escaped from captivity and found Jean. Jean made them believe that Nadia still had the Blue Water, so both joined forces to rescue her. They destroyed the power plant that powered the laser weapon by blocking its water supply. They also recovered Gratan, which was hidden in the forest, and used it to assault the castle and rescue Nadia, Marie, and King.

While escaping using Gratan, the enemy's submarine (**Garfish**) blocked their way. Unexpectedly, the Nautilus emerged and attacked the submarine. Gargoyle tried to use the laser weapon to destroy the Nautilus but, due to the lack of energy, it exploded and destroyed the whole base. The Nautilus and Gratan managed to escape to the sea and Gargoyle escaped on a zeppelin.

The Nautilus rescued Gratan and its crew. Gargoyle set a trap for the Nautilus using submarine mines, but Hanson, Sanson, and Jean removed some mines using Gratan, so the Nautilus could escape. Nemo explained Jean that the Nautilus was powered with a particle annihilation engine, which was very advanced technology, but very dangerous in the wrong hands.

## Atlantis

Grandis fell in love with captain Nemo and tried to please him. However, Nemo's first officer, **Electra**, was also secretely in love with him.

A few days later the Nautilus arrived to an uninhabited island, where they gathered food and stayed for a while.

Grandis explained Nadia and Marie that her family got rich with diamond mines. Her mother died when she was young. When she was 16, she met a handsome italian man, **Gonzalez**, and later they announce their engagement despite her father's disapproval. When Grandis' father agreed to pay her dauther's fiancée's debts, he lost most of his fortune, and he stole everything else. Grandis and her father lost their home. Her friends left her, except for their chauffeur Sanson and their mechanic Hanson. They survived by selling her mother's jewels. Since then, she became obsessed with jewels.

The Nautilus crew discovered that Neo-Atlantean soldiers had arrived to the island with the Garfish. They almost capture Marie, but Sanson found and rescued her. Nemo ordered the pursue of the Garfish. After 10 days of pursue, the Nautilus almost had the Garfish within firing range. However, Marie and Nadia began to suffer from Tropical fever, and the doctor predicted that they would be dead in two days. Nemo decided to let go the Garfish, despite Electra's opposition, and headed the Nautilus to reef 64 to get the antidote for Tropical fever. This saved Marie and Nadia.

The Nautilus continued its pursue and eventually got close to the Garfish again. The Garfish attacked with magnetic bombs, which damaged the Nautilus, and torpedos, which the Nautilus evaded. Then the Garfish crashed the Nautilus, but the Nautilus was too strong and resisted while the Garfish was completely destroyed.

After the combat, the Nautilus emerged. However, following the advice of a Neo-Atlantean, the USS captain Melville (at the USS Barracuda) had brought 4 warships to the area. At Nautilus sight, they attacked the submarine, which suffered more damages. The Nautilus submerged and let them think it was sunk. The attack caused the death of 3 crew members from toxic gas leak at the engine room. When the warships were gone, it emerged and was repaired.

After traveling for a while, Nemo used his own Blue Water to open a secret passage at the bottom of the sea, and the Nautilus entered into **Atlantis**. It was a huge hidden bubble, a continent that vanished from the Earth 12.000 years ago. Now it was an uninhabited land with still visible remnants of an advanced civilization. The Nautilus crew used to get to Atlantis to bury those who die fighting Gargoyle. There they buried the three fallen sailors. Jean discovered that his father was dead after meeting a crew member that was the only survivor of the ship captained by Jean's father, which was destroyed by the Garfish.

## Antartica

Nemo sent a landing party to find out the latest news about the Neo-Atlanteans, but there were no news. Apparently, they had gone undercover. Then the Nautilus headed to its base, in Antartica, to get maintenance materials. During the trip, they passed close to submarine volcanoes and were attacked by a giant shellfish. After passing through a secret underground passage, the Nautilus got to its hidden base, a huge cavern under the North pole. There was the Nautilus' home port, built by the Atlanteans in a subterranean lake. The Nautilus underwent various repairs there.

Nemo took Nadia and Jean through a passage. They could see some frozen things: a **World tree**, extinct animals, and **saurians**. Then he introduced his friend **Irion**, a 20.000 years old white whale, to Nadia and Jean. Using the Blue Water, Nadia was able to talk with Irion, who said that he was too old and would die soon. Irion added that Nadia had a brother, and that she would soon be someone else. Then Nemo took the kids to the surface, where they saw an aurora. Nemo explained the kids a few things:

- World tree: Huge tree whose roots spread throughout the globe, and it's said to be the source of all life.
- Saurians: First Earth inhabitants, now extinct. They had the ability to adapt their physiology to environmental changes. They established a civilization on Earth long before men. But 70 million years ago the sun bombarded Earth with cosmic rays, and they vanished forever.
- Aurora: It's created by electrons and protons from the sun striking the atmosphere.
- Atlanteans lived 20.000 years ago.

## Neo-Atlanteans

Meanwhile, the New-Atlanteans had gained control of a relatively small part of the world economy, and were building Garships, aerial warships, and another super weapon (Tower of Babel).

Once repaired and ready, the Nautilus went back to open sea. There it detected a Garfish, which attacked and destroyed.

Electra explained Nadia that 13 years ago, Gargoyle attacked his town, destroyed it, and killed his brother. Nemo rescued her from Gargoyle. The survivors built the Nautilus to defeat Gargoyle and his followers.

Gargoyle set a trap for the Nautilus. Many Garfishes surrounded the Nautilus and, since the seabed only had 300 meters depth, this forced the Nautilus to sail into the Kermodec trench. The Garfishes attacked with torpedos, but the Nautilus evaded them with countermeasures and descending to the seabed. The Nautilus counterattacked with depth charges and missiles, destroying 10 Garfishes, and the others retreated. A huge aerial warship (more like a space battleship) used a big powerful magnet to catch the Nautilus from above and take it out of the water. Then it used and atomic vibrator and supersonic wave gun to cause very serious damage to the Nautilus. The Nautilus responded with a volley, but the warship had an invisible shield and received no damage. The Garfishes attacked the Nautilus from the sea surface. The warship attacked using big bombs, which required making an opening on the shield. This allowed the Grandis Gang, from Gratan, and the Nautilus, to shot one of the bombs right before it could leave the warship. This resulted in the destruction of the big magnet and the Nautilus falling into the sea. Gratan fell far away from the combat area.

Under the sea, the Garfishes attacked the Nautilus with depth charges. At 2400 meters depth, the Nautilus executed the separation of its combat block (with Nemo, Electra, Nadia, Jean, Marie, King, and a few crew members) and its central block (the rest of the crew). Nemo released the particle annihilation engine of the Nautilus and made it explode, destroying the Garfish fleet. At 4000 meters depth, the Nautilus was out of Gargoyle's range and was taken by a deep sea current.

Nemo sent the crew to the subordinate bridge. He also sent the kids (Nadia, Jean, Marie, King) to the Captain's cabin and locked them there since he wanted to jettison it from the main block. Electra was furious with Nemo and almost kill him with a pistol. She explained a few things:

- In the past, the Kingdom of **Tartessos** was ruled by Nemo (king **Eleusis La Arwall**) and his queen. But 13 years ago, a group of rebels leaded by Gargoyle staged a coup d'état that began with the assasination of the queen. Gargoyle seized power and changed the kingdom's name to Neo-Atlantis.
- He had the support of **Emperor Neo**, who wanted to unlock the power of the Tower of Babel. However, when they activated the tower with the Blue Water, it destroyed the entire kingdom. This caused the dead of Electra's brother, mother, and father. Later, Nemo found her alone and rescued her.
- Five years later, Electra eavesdropped on a Nemo's conversation and learned that the kingdom's destruction was caused by Nemo. Nemo, to prevent Gargoyle from devastating the world, pulled the Blue Water out of the control device, causing the tower to self-destruct.
- Electra was in love with Nemo and followed him in order to kill Gargoyle. However, Nemo changed since he met his own daughter, Nadia. He became more like a father than a determined fighter.

Nadia heard Electra's words and realized Nemo was his father right before Nemo jettisoned the Captain's cabin.

## Red Noah

The cabin emerged and was drifted ashore a deserted island. There they found the remnants of a Garfish destroyed at least one month ago. Initially, they survived eating the food from the Captain's cabin until it run out, then they caught fishes on the sea. Jean built a seawater distillation apparatus in order to get drinking water. Nadia tried to live alone in nature, but after a while she discovered that wasn't possible and returned with the others. Nadia and Jean kissed each other and became a couple. Nadia didn't want to eat animals, so she ate rotten food from the Garfish, but she fell ill after that. Jean made food for her containing meat, without she knowing. One day, a typhoon destroyed most of what they had built.

They saw an island moving next to the kid's island, and they went there using a boat built by Jean. There they met the Grandis Gang, and also Ayerton, a crew member Nadia and Jean met in the warship that rescued them.

- Ayerton explained his story: After the sea monster attacked his ship, they discovered that the monster was actually a submarine. They found the submarine once more and attacked it, which caused the ship to shake, and he fell to the sea. The fleet left him behind. He drifted for days on top of some barrels until he arrived to the floating island.
- The Grandis Gang explained their story: While figthing Gargoyle, Gratan fell far away from the combat area, and they drifted ashore and landed on the floating island.

The kids decided to stay on the floating island with the others. Later, the island moved away from the kids' island. The island used to move from 1 to 10 kms/day. Everybody lived peacefully in the island for about 4 months. One day, Jean discovered that the island was made of metal underground. Another day, he and Nadia discovered a metallic tunnel and entered into it, discovering that the whole island was actually a very advanced ship. An AI took Nadia alone into the complex and explained her a few things:

- That place was the satelite city **Red Noah** (the Holy Fortress).
- The people of Atlantis came from the M78 nebula (277.5 light years from Earth). They made an emergency landing on Earth 2.4 million years ago. They were unable to return to their own star, so they lived on Earth and created a powerful civilization.
- The Atlanteans waged Holy Wars that caused the destruction of their great cities. The few survivors built a new civilization in the continent of **Pangea**, but it was destroyed again by war.
- Nadia was the inheritor of the Blue Water and heir of **Taurus Mephistos**. She is not human, but the heir of the Atlantean dynasty of the M78 nebula. She must now become the ruler of Atlantis in order to guide others toward its restoration.

The Blue Water (also called the Philosopher's stone) of Nadia activated the ship and the AI proceded to submerge the island 800 meters, in the Indian Ocean. Nadia didn't want to stay, so she escaped the island with her friends on the Gratan using a balloon they made. Gargoyle detected a high energy reading from and a Garfish approached to the sunken island.

## Tartessos

The Gratan arrived to Africa. They landed in a village, and its people welcomed them after realizing Nadia had the Blue Water. Nadie befriended **Hamahama**, who explained that, in the past, there was the **Silver Kingdom** beyond the village, and its ruler had a Blue Water too. Its ruler gave the village a silver column (actually, canned food) as a sign of friendship. However, a few years ago a big war destroyed the kingdom. Many people (europeans, americans, arabians, etc.) have tried to steal the column from the village, but it has always defended it successfully. However, when nobody was watching, King opened the can and ate the food.

King was captured by Gonzalez (Grandis' former fiancé), a treasure hunter who was after the silver column. He asked Hamahama to exchange King for the silver column; otherwise, he would kill King after 24 hours. Grandis and Nadia tried to rescue King, but they were captured too. In a joint attack, the others and Hamahama rescued King, Nadia, and Grandis.

Hamahama's fiancé told them where Tartessos was, and the party went there flying on the Gratan. They arrived to a lake in the mountains, and Nadia used the Blue Water to open the waters and get into a building under the lake with her friends and the Gratan. She explained that the Blue Water can be very dangerous and that only those related to the royal family (like Nadia) can handle it. They discovered there many jails with dead animals.

Nadia explained that humans know that place as Noah's Ark, from the Old Testament. She added that, long time ago, the people of Atlantis fought and killed each other. Atlantis was an ark that was burned and sunk into the sea. Red Noah (the Floating island) was another ark that lost its master and disappeared. Another ark, the **Blue Noah**, lost its power to fly and fell there. Only the people in the Blue Noah survived, and they established the Kingdom of Tartessos not far from the ark. Nadia is the princess and heir of Tartessos.

Nadia could use the Blue Water to activate Blue Noah and make use of its great power, a power so big that could be used to destroy the world. They got to Tartessos, which was a city in the middle of a lake, and reached the top of the Tower of Babel, in the middle of the city. Nadia explained that the tower is an apparatus for communicating with her real homeland: M78. Nadia was overwhelmed by the great power she inherited and, to prevent her from making bad use of it, she jumped from the tower to kill herself, but the Blue Water saved her by slowing down her fall. She discovered her date of birth in a stone stele (31/05/1875), which meant that it was her birthday that very day.

Two Gargoyle's space battleships appeared over Tartessos and detected Nadia. Nadia surrendered to Gargoyle to save her friends, and she was taken to a battleship. Nadia's friends ran away, got into the Gratan, and tried to escape underground, but Gargoyle was bombing above them. The Gratan fell off a cliff, but suddenly Electra, on a flying ship, rescued them and took to another battleship, **New Nautilus** (originally called **Excelion**), a legendary battleship hidden underground for 12.000 years. Nemo and his crew welcomed them inside. They had found the ship in Tartessos underground, and made it work.

Gargoyle detected Nemo's ship and bombed the area. The Excelion emerged from underground and destroyed one battleship with a single blow using its powerful annihilation weapon. Gargoyle demanded that Nemo hand over his own Blue Water in order to save Nadia. Nemo was thinking about that when his Blue Water disappeared from his hand. This revealed that Emperor Neo was with Gargoyle, since only Neo had the power to do that. Then Gargole bombed Tartessos, apparently destroying the Excelion.

Gargoyle introduced Nadia to Emperor Neo, who now had Nemo's and Nadia's Blue Waters. Neo was supporting Gargoyle, who wanted Neo to help Neo Atlantis rise once again. Gargoyle planned to declare war on the world. In order to restore Atlantis' control over the world, Gargoyle made Red Noah rise above the sea.

The Excelion survived by accessing a great underground tunnel made by the Atlanteans. Tunnels like that were all around the world, under all five continents and seven oceans. Electra explained that the old Nautilus was saved when it was broken because it was carried by an underwater tide to that tunnel.

Red Noah, Nautilus, and Excelion were spaceships at first, when the Atlanteans landed 2.4 million years ago. Both Red Noah and Excelion have the same powerful main engine (a large-sized Orpheus type)

Gargoyle explained Nadia, and Electra explained Jean, that Atlanteans created the humans to be their servants using genetics. At Red Noah, Gargoyle showed Nadia the first human, **Adam**, a huge experimental human body. Over time they were able to reduce its size until they got its present size. They first tried to make whales their servants (like Irion, at Antartica), but failed. Next, they used primates, gave them high intelligence, and made them look like Altanteans themselves.

Neo revealed Nadia that he was his brother, and thus the true heir of the Blue Water. He let Nadia escaped 13 years ago because he didn't want to kill his sister.

The Neo-Atlanteans revealed their presence to the world by placing Red Noah above Paris, and using big holographic images of Neo worldwide. Neo revealed that he was the emperor of Neo-Atlantis, and that atlanteans created the humanity. He demanded humans to obey them and become their servants. As a show of force, he destroyed one city.

Once the Excelion was ready, Nemo left his crew on land and got prepared for going to battle alone. However, a bunch of crew members joined him anyway, including the Grandis Gang and Jean. Excelion went to Paris and fought Red Noah. Nemo rammed his ship into Red Noah to break their invisible shield, but failed, and his ship was thrown at the Eiffel Tower. He tried again with the shield activated, and this time the ship made a hole in Red Noah's shield and attacked, making a hole in its hull. The Grandis Gand aboard the Gratan infiltrated Red Noah by jumping to the shield's hole and into the hull's hole.

Red Noah ascended to the space closely followed by Excelion. Red Noah attacked Excelion with his Tower of Babel. Excelion activated its damaged shield, which deviated part of the attack, but the shield was broken and the ship was hit. Excelion survived the hit, but the shield was left unusable. When Red Noah was ready to use the tower again to deliver the final blow, the Grandis Gang made Gratan explode in the tower, destroying it. The Grandis Gang was captured shortly after.

Excelion rammed again, attacked the hull's hole even more, and penetrated into Red Noah through that hole. Nemo, Electra, and Jean disembarked inside Red Noah. Gargoyle met them and Nemo revealed that Gargoyle was the Prime Minister of Tartessos, **Nemesis La Algol**. Neo (**Venusis**) and Nadia were now Gargoyle's puppets. He had taken the consciousness away from Nadia. Neo was destroyed 13 year ago by the Tower of Babel, but Gargoyle used Atlantean technology to rebuild him into an obedient cyborg.

At Gargoyle's command, Nadia and Neo shot Nemo with a gun, leaving him seriously hurt. Electra secretely contacted the New Nautilus, gave it their position, and asked for help. The New Nautilus fired at that position, damaging Neo and its control system, which made him recover his consciousness. Neo then broke the control system of Nadia, making her recover consciousness, but Gargoyle disconnected Neo, which killed him.

Nadia made Red Noah rise up very high in order to destroy it later by letting if fall.
