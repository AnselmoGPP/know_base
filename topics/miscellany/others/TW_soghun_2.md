## Campaign advices

- Keep happiness of your cities positive. Otherwise, you will have to fight rebellions.
- You can loot other cities, but other kingdoms won't like you. You may end up being hated by everybody (dangerous).
- Build facilities:
  - Fortified cities (fort > stronghold > fortress > castle > citadel) are more defendable, but consume more food.
  - Some facilities produce more food (improved irrigation > terrace farming > land consolidation). Building them may require studying certain arts (Todofuken, Equal fields…).
  - Some facilities give happiness (sake den > gambling hall > criminal syndicate).
  - Some facilities consume food (rice exchange > merchant guild > kabunakama), but offer other benefits.

Basic buildings:

- Fort: More population, public order, and defense. Less food.
  - Fort > Stronghold > Fortress > Castle > Citadel

- Rice paddies: More food.
  - Rice paddies > Improved irrigation > Terrace farming > Land consolidation

- Market:
  - Market > Rice exchange > Merchant guild > Kabunakama
  
- Temple: More happiness.
  - Buddhist temple > Monastery > Temple complex > Famous temple
  - Chappel > Mission/Hospital > Church/Jesuit seminary > Cathedral/Jesuit college

- Sake den: More happiness.
  - Sake den > Gambling hall > Criminal syndicate > Infamous Mizu Shobai district

- School: Less corruption.
  - School > Library/Magistrate > Confucian academy/Law court
  
- Trails: More speed.
  - Trails > Roads > Post roads and stations > Imperial roads and towers
